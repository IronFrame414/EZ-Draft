# Phase 8 · Detail editor

Goal: draw custom 2D details with parts, hatches, line roles, keynotes and steps, place them on sheets, and extrude closed profiles to 3D.

**Build gate:** the browser test draws the floating-shelf detail, places it on A5.0 after print preview with its “3 / A5.0” callout, and extrudes its profile. **Field check (Josh):** draw a detail from a current job.
