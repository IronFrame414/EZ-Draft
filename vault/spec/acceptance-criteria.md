Generated by `vault/tools/gen_acs.mjs` from each ticket's Done-when (its row in `vault/tickets/phases/phase-NN.md`); do not hand-edit generated rows. One row per testable sentence, `AC-<ticket>-<n>`. Rows marked *(field check)* are Josh's manual checks at the phase field check, not automated tests.
