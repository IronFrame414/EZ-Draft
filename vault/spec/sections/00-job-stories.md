# 00 · Job stories (Round 1 interview, Josh, 2026-10-20)

How Worth Properties runs a job today and where EZ Draft fits, in Josh's words as confirmed by him on 2026-10-20 (vault/questions/interview-plan-2026-10-20.md, Round 1). The decisions this round made are Q-0177 to Q-0196 in `vault/questions/QUESTIONS.md`; they override older spec text where they differ.

## What EZ Draft is for (Q-0177)

A clean, measured plan the crew can build from, plus a permit set when the job needs one. It also helps clients decide on selections: AI photoreal renders show the new design with the exact items they are choosing between. Any layout can be built from scratch or changed. Every feature is built for v1; nothing is held for v2. EZ Binder (a separate app) handles intake, estimates, change-order signing and closeout paperwork; EZ Draft has no connection to it in v1 (Q-0190).

## Who does what (Q-0178)

- Jobs come in by referral, phone call or website submission.
- Any staff member can start a project, including crew-level staff (for example a returning client calls for more work and Josh sends whoever is free).
- Josh or the foreman usually measures, but anyone may. Measuring and drawing are not tied to one person: anyone can start, and the same person or someone else finishes, in any order.
- In the middle of a job, any staff member may be sent to re-measure or add measurements of specific items.
- What each person can do is set by permissions Josh can change at any time.

## Measure

- Polycam on the iPhone gets most measurements and the layout close enough.
- The important measurements are taken by hand and written down.
- Kitchens: room layout, existing cabinets and appliances, ceiling height.
- Whoever measured draws it, or the Polycam drawing is used.

## Job 1 · Kitchen

- Design: Josh connects the client with a designer, or uses a cabinet company's 3D design system.
- Client approval: decided by the scope of work; some clients are allowed to skip it.
- Changes: cabinet layout, moved walls, moved appliances. Example (a job in progress): an 8 × 8 island cut down to 6 × 8, so the layout is redone and a new quartzite slab may be selected.
- The crew needed: cabinet layout elevations and renderings; before the cabinets went in, appliance specs and locations, locations of appliance electric and gas connections, plumbing locations and outlet locations.
- The job ends when the scope of work is completed.

## Job 2 · Bath

- Tile layout, which needs client sign-off; tile type.
- Plumbing locations, vanity drawings, niche details.
- Permit drawings need shower pan details and drawings.

## Job 3 · Electrical, plumbing and the permit set

- Plumbing is added or moved regularly; sometimes a whole house is re-plumbed. Josh knows plumbing less well than electrical, so the plumbing rules need research (Q-0179).
- Load calculation is needed for a panel change, a service change or new circuits. Today Josh checks common loads and researches them again every time; the common loads should be data in the system (Q-0181).
- Josh submits to several building departments, mostly within Palm Beach County, including unincorporated Palm Beach County; they ask for much the same things (Q-0193).
- A permit submittal needs: current layout, proposed layout, electrical layout, electrical load calculation, plumbing locations, plumbing diagrams and possibly plumbing calculations, mechanical drawings and calculations.
- Past rejections: missing details on the drawings; drawings not matching the scope of work. Example (Boca Raton): the scope listed electrical work the client had removed, and the drawings had no electrical (Q-0180).
- An engineer or architect is involved generally only for structural changes. Moving a wall is technically structural; Josh draws minor items but EZ Draft builds no structural calculations (Q-0182).

## Changes and closeout

- Changes after approval are drawn as revisions; for pricing and signing, Josh downloads a Scope PDF from EZ Draft (written scope plus before/after drawings or renderings) and attaches it to an estimate or change order in EZ Binder (Q-0190). Pricing stays in EZ Draft as the spec has it (Q-0196).
- Closeout is a simple step in EZ Draft with nothing that blocks it. Some projects need one simple drawing, others dozens (Q-0191).
