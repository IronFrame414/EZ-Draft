| Q-0568 | 2026-11-04 | Agent-settled phase 4 gaps (orchestrator, spec-check 4; worked examples 09j). Plan closure uses the fixed 1/64" comparison, inclusive (03b rule 5, 03g); the owner's Measurement tolerance covers scan conflicts, field measurements and the 17b closure check only (S15d), so a 1/2" gap always shows (12 Room closure). The 12" limit of Q-0562 is inclusive. Only a wall parallel to the gap can absorb it, since "that wall's length changes" (S02); a wall across the gap is refused with a message. A room kept out of square: area closes the outline across the gap; who, when and the gap are stored on the room; the override clears when a later edit changes the gap. room.closure_overridden is emitted through the event registry now and reaches the event log with EZD-109 (EZD-022 Scope), and joins section 07's event table. A length must be above zero and at most 200 ft, the 17b sanity range (S15d). Room area rounds to whole square feet with halves going up (Q-0207; 03g's halfway rule). Gap sizes display to 1/16", so a small gap never reads 0" (S02 board "5/8" gap"). |
| Q-0569 | 2026-11-04 | Round 2 (Josh, chat: "Accept all 4"). (a) Snap increment 1/2" until the snap settings arrive (EZD-219, phase 6). (c) The 12" limit of Q-0562 is the straight-line distance. A miss in both directions offers only Keep out of square, with a line saying to fix the walls once move arrives in phase 5. (spec-check 4) |
| Q-0570 | 2026-11-04 | Agent-settled round 2 gaps (orchestrator, spec-check 4; worked examples 09j). Limits: above zero and at most 200 ft apply only to wall and room fields (wall length, Room rectangle, template sizes), are checked after rounding to 1/16", and are a fixed refusal set by Q-0568, not the 17b confirm prompt; other length fields accept 0" (03g row 9). Turn angle: the first wall measures from the page's horizontal; 0 up to but not including 180 is accepted, decimals as typed; 180, above it and negatives are refused. Snapping: all types on until EZD-219; capture within 10 screen pixels and 5°; priority endpoints, midpoints, wall faces (the finished face), 90/45°, increment; 90/45° measure from the last wall, or the page for a first wall; "where the cursor points" (Q-0560) means after snapping. Gap: shown only for a chain of three or more walls; a gap over 1/64" that rounds to 0" displays as 1/16" (replaces Q-0568's "never reads 0"" reasoning). A move or absorb that would leave a wall at zero or over 200 ft is refused. Walls of other rooms cannot be picked (S02 Decisions). A wall dividing a room makes two rooms; a wall crossing a wall joins at the crossing. Room rectangle grows from the clicked corner toward the cursor, right and down if the cursor has not moved; Width is across the screen, Depth is up and down. Lengths also accept 12 1/2, 6-1/2", 6.5" and 12' 6, and curly quote marks as tablet keyboards type them; anything else is refused. |
