| Q-0568 | 2026-11-04 | Agent-settled phase 4 gaps (orchestrator, spec-check 4; worked examples 09j). Plan closure uses the fixed 1/64" comparison, inclusive (03b rule 5, 03g); the owner's Measurement tolerance covers scan conflicts, field measurements and the 17b closure check only (S15d), so a 1/2" gap always shows (12 Room closure). The 12" limit of Q-0562 is inclusive. Only a wall parallel to the gap can absorb it, since "that wall's length changes" (S02); a wall across the gap is refused with a message. A room kept out of square: area closes the outline across the gap; who, when and the gap are stored on the room; the override clears when a later edit changes the gap. room.closure_overridden is emitted through the event registry now and reaches the event log with EZD-109 (EZD-022 Scope), and joins section 07's event table. A length must be above zero and at most 200 ft, the 17b sanity range (S15d). Room area rounds to whole square feet with halves going up (Q-0207; 03g's halfway rule). Gap sizes display to 1/16", so a small gap never reads 0" (S02 board "5/8" gap"). |
