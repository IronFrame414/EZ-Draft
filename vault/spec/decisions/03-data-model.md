| Q-0488 | 2026-11-02 | Keep mine / Keep theirs is built in phase 3, as new ticket EZD-294 "Conflict list: Keep mine / Keep theirs" (after EZD-120b and EZD-202); cost: one more ticket in phase 3. When the save route refuses a queued change for good, the change leaves the head of the outbox for a conflict list on that device, later edits keep saving and nothing is lost; each row is resolved with Keep mine (send the queued change over the server's current object) or Keep theirs (drop the queued change), Architecture rule 9. A change for a project that is gone or no longer open to the user has no "mine" to keep; its row says why and offers only removing it.
