## Phase 12 · Client link

Goal: the homeowner views renders and plans on a phone, comments, chooses options and edges, verifies, signs and approves, and gets a selections PDF.

**Build gate:** the Client acceptance test passes in a phone-size browser test (signature logs name, date and IP; approval locks the version; the link expires at 30 days and renews), and the client page meets its download-size budget with no editor or 3D code. **Field check (Josh):** a real client approves selections from a phone.
