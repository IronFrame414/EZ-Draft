## Phase 16 · Catalog + takeoff

Goal: a full product catalog filled by link, pasted text or screenshot with confirmed AI fields and project price snapshots, and takeoff quantities by room and trade with waste factors.

**Build gate:** products are added from recorded link, pasted-text and screenshot fixtures with every field confirmed; the takeoff CSV for the fixture kitchen matches its hand count; the Permissions acceptance test passes (unticking See company costs removes every company cost, and unticking See sell prices every sell price, markup and total, from that user's views and exports). **Field check (Josh):** add ten real products and check a takeoff against a real kitchen.
