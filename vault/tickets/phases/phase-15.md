## Phase 15 · Translations

Goal: AI-drafted Spanish for notes, keynotes and field tasks, reviewed in one queue and reused across jobs, with Spanish users always seeing Spanish.

**Build gate:** signed in as a Spanish-language user, the fixture field pack shows no English task or note text, and the Translations acceptance test passes (editing an approved phrase updates every use; the hold setting holds only set issue). **Field check (Josh):** a Spanish-speaking crew member reads the whole field pack.
