## Phase 14 · Field pack + offline + field measurements review

Goal: crews use a room-by-room phone field pack offline, and the office approves field measurements before they change the model; the lock holder can edit offline without loss.

**Build gate:** the Offline and Field acceptance tests pass in browser tests with the network switched off and on (the holder edits offline and reconnects with every queued change in a new version; a field measurement changes the model only after approval; a conflict notifies the office), and the field pack meets its download-size budget. **Field check (Josh):** a crew uses the field pack on a live job for a week.
