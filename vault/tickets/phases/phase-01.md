## Phase 1 · Foundation

Goal: users, permissions, projects, customers, versions, the edit lock, notifications and the event log work end to end before any drawing tool exists.

**Build gate:** against the EZ Binder stub (EZD-019), create a project from a stub job; pass the Edit lock acceptance test with two browser sessions (amber banner with holder and time; Request edit prompts the holder; no answer for the release time saves and releases; banner turns green); the Projects page shows its first cards within budget with 200 seeded projects. **Field check (Josh):** repeat the EZ Binder steps against the real EZ Binder once its side of the contract is built.

**Removed from v1 (Q-0190, 2026-10-20):** EZD-114, EZD-115, EZD-119. The rows stay below as the text for the later EZ Binder connector (BACKLOG.md); they are not in tickets.json or STATUS.md.
