## Phase 3 · Imports

Goal: bring a Polycam scan, a PDF or a DXF into the model through Import review, with typed and laser values overriding the scan.

**Build gate:** the Import acceptance test passes on the fixture Polycam scan, vector PDF and DXF (low-confidence items highlighted; nothing enters the model before accept; a typed override lists its conflicts). **Field check (Josh):** import a real Polycam scan from a current job and fix it.
