# EZD-269 · Fix: a wait on another ticket is a dependency, and the ticket resumes by itself

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-269-1, AC-EZD-269-2, AC-EZD-269-3, AC-EZD-269-4
- **Complexity:** high (section 8)
- **Areas:** tooling, scripts, ledger, process
- **User-visible result:** a ticket that waits on another ticket goes back in the queue with that ticket as a dependency and builds again by itself once it is done; a red merge that is develop's fault creates the wait instead of costing a round; merge conflicts and a dirty develop are handled by agents, never left for Josh.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-29 · Status: split proposal
