# EZD-204b · Offsets, hosting and the Not on a wall flag

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-204-1, AC-EZD-204-2, AC-EZD-204-3, AC-EZD-204-4, AC-EZD-204-7, AC-EZD-204-8, AC-EZD-204-9, AC-EZD-204-10, AC-EZD-204-11, AC-EZD-204-12, AC-EZD-204-13, AC-EZD-204-15

- **User-visible result:** When a wall edit leaves a door or window off every wall, it stays where it was and draws in error red on the plan; it clears when a wall touches it again, in the same undo step. Doors draw with their own hinge end and swing side.
