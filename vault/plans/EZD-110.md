## Split proposal

The whole ticket touches about 24 source files before tests (4 migrations, job kind and handler, server module, 4 routes, dev page, bell, panel, store, time format, tokens, catalogs, harness lists, docs) plus 11 icon SVGs, the sprite and the names file, and needs about 30 tests.
