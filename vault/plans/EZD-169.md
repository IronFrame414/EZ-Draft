# EZD-169 · Fix: every 500 catch in a route reports the error

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-169-1 (`vault/spec/acceptance-criteria.md` row 326, generated from Done-when)
- **Complexity:** low (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** nothing on screen; when the database fails behind the job, Realtime token or health address, Josh now gets the error in Sentry and the new-error email, instead of nothing.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: planned · Patched 2026-10-26 (test-author PLAN-GAP: permission-context mock, T6 (iv), T7 accepts `reportError(`, line numbers on develop `5cee3ff`)

## 1. What to build

Data: none (no migration, no table, no money field).

Item 53 of `vault/tickets/sweep-2026-10-24.md` lists the four routes that answer `500 db_unavailable` without calling `reportError`, breaking the convention in docs/architecture.md `### Error reporting (EZD-024)` ("a `catch` that turns an unexpected error into a 500 calls `reportError`"). A grep of `status: 500` over `route.ts` files in `app/src` (develop `5cee3ff`, 2026-10-26) finds six files. Two already call `reportError` in their 500 catch and are out of scope (Do not touch): `app/src/app/api/permissions/route.ts` (location `api.permissions`) and `app/src/app/api/dev/permissions/projects/[id]/route.ts` (location `api.dev.permissions`), both EZD-102. The other four, with five catch blocks, are this ticket's:

Q-0318 (a) moves that change from EZD-181 into this ticket; EZD-181 keeps only the timeout tests.
