# EZD-208 · Selection (split proposal)

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-208-1 to AC-EZD-208-18, shared out to the parts below
- **Complexity:** high (each part; section 8)
- **Areas:** ui, testing
- **User-visible result:** Select (V) is the first rail tool and is on when the plan opens. A click picks the smallest wall or door under the cursor, clicking again at the same spot steps out to the wall, Shift-click adds or removes, a box selects (left to right: wholly inside; right to left: touching), Ctrl/Cmd+A selects everything, and Esc clears. Selected objects turn cobalt and hover shows what a click would pick. View-only users can select too. On a tablet, Select more and Select all are on-screen buttons.
- Phase: 5 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-07 · Status: split proposal

## Split proposal
**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket touches about 38 files: 26 source, doc and generated icon files (icon pipeline 6, tokens 2), 6 new test files and 9 earlier test files that change (the rail grows a tool, view-only users get a rail, the hover mode and the key listener move). It also needs about 30 tests. Both are over the size rule (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files or 25 tests; Josh 2026-10-22). The cut follows EZD-204's: the logic first (pure modules and the canvas seams, unit-tested), then the screen (React, rail, keys, tablet bar, browser tests). Sections 1 to 10 below hold the decisions both parts share and the seam contract; each part's plan takes them unchanged and adds its own Build steps detail, tests and checklist. Build order a → b.

| D1 Candidates | Walls and openings on the open floor that the drawing list draws (an opening it omits for `host_not_found` or an empty span is not pickable). Rooms are never candidates until EZD-218. | Q-0630 row 7 ("smallest plan footprint, ties to first id"); Q-0630 row 8; S03 Click ("under the cursor"); P207b hit test (10 px, `hover.ts` `pickWall`); `G/drawing/build.ts` opening placement |
