# EZD-175 · Parallel lanes: a ticket branch is brought up to date with develop before its merge

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-175-1, AC-EZD-175-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** when two builds finish close together, the second one first takes in develop's newest changes and is re-tested in its own folder before it lands on develop, so two edits to different parts of docs/architecture.md no longer stop a build; only edits to the same lines stop, before anything reaches develop.
