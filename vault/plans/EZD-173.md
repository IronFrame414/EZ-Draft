- **ACs:** AC-EZD-173-1, AC-EZD-173-2, AC-EZD-173-3

### Do not touch
`scripts/gate.mjs` (EZD-172), `vault/tools/boost.mjs`, `scripts/budget.mjs`, RUN's scheduling and budget wiring (EZD-000h C12 order; EZD-174 adds lanes), NT logic other than its four writes, the skills of phase-wide units (questions, review-plans, gate-phase, wrap: they run alone, EZD-174 row), `.claude/hooks/guard-*.mjs`, `stop-ledger-check.mjs`, existing test assertions except those section 4 lists.

Ticket row: **Scope** Units commit all of vault/ (scripts/run.mjs commitVault, scripts/unit-steps.mjs finish), merges squash onto develop and run the develop gate in the one main checkout, and STATUS.md and QUESTIONS.md are rewritten whole, so two units at once could lose each other's writes or merge at the same moment. Add one ledger lock for every write and commit of vault/ ledgers, have each unit commit only its own files, serialize merges to develop and the develop gate behind a merge lock, and give each unit its own in-flight record. Guarded files follow PROCESS.md rule 19. **Done when** Two units finishing at the same moment both keep their STATUS and QUESTIONS changes (test); two merges queue and both land with green develop gates (test); a unit's commit holds only its own files. **Depends on** EZD-000g, EZD-000h, EZD-001. **Spec ref** Josh 2026-10-24 (boost).
