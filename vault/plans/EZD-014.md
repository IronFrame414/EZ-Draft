### Level-1 answers recorded here (not questions)
- **What "simple layout" means.** One column with a header bar, one main area and an optional bottom bar; no rail, left panel, inspector or status bar. Sources: 02 Platform ("No full drafting on phone"); S17/S18 Layout ("Phone 390x844. Not the editor shell"); S07 Decisions 8 ("full-screen viewport … no inspector"); the common frame of all five phone boards.
- **iPad landscape and the Surface get the full shell at the spec's fixed sizes.** No source shrinks or collapses the rail (56), left panel (248) or inspector (336) on a tablet (04 Frame; 02 Platform "full editor"; Q-0088). The canvas takes what is left: canvas width = W − 720 (16 + 56 + 16 + 248 + 16 + 16 + 336 + 16). It is 880 at 1600 (EZD-012 B1), 460 at the iPad's 1180 and 720 at the Surface's 1440 (Q-0053), and 304 at the 1024 breakpoint (Q-0054 (a)).
- **"44 px" is the touch target, on touch devices.** 11 says "iPad touch targets at least 44 px"; Q-0088 builds and tests the full editor for the Surface the same way, so the rule applies to the Surface too. The desktop boards draw 40 px rail buttons and about 38 px header buttons and are "final and approved", so a mouse-only desktop keeps the board sizes and the 44 px rule applies where a touch screen is present. Detection: CSS `@media (any-pointer: coarse)` (*planner's choice*: `any-pointer` rather than `pointer`, so an iPad with a keyboard and trackpad, or a Surface with its Type Cover attached, still gets finger-sized targets; a Windows touch screen reports `any-pointer: coarse` whether or not a mouse or trackpad is also present — *confirm in Chromium emulation*: B8 asserts it first in EZD-016a's `surface` project). Phones match the same query, so phone screens get 44 px too (harmless; consistent for EZD-1404 and EZD-1013).
- **A target is the tappable area, not the drawn box.** Where layout leaves room (rail, header buttons, tabs, wordmark) the element box itself is at least 44 × 44. Where the visual must stay smaller (project name inside the 58 px header, later status-bar controls inside the 30 px bar) an invisible hit-area extension makes the tappable area 44 × 44 (*planner's choice*; the 30 px bar is 04's value and is not changed).
- **Phone strings are `field.` keys in the user's language.** The phone notice is a crew phone surface (R26 R17), lives in `components/phone/` and is linted there (EZD-026a Step 4). The language comes from the user record (03 `user`; EZD-026b); in this ticket the dev page takes it as a parameter.
- **Hover styles do not stick on touch.** iPad Safari keeps `:hover` after a tap. Tooltips and hover backgrounds of the shell are wrapped in `@media (hover: hover)` so a tapped rail tool does not keep a tooltip or hover tint (*planner's choice*; 04 says the tooltip shows on "hovering"). Keyboard focus still shows the tooltip (EZD-012 Step 2). A Surface with a trackpad attached reports `hover: hover`, so it keeps hover styles for the trackpad; how Edge treats a finger tap there is checked at the field check (Risks).
- **The icon-only header form (Q-0055 (a)).** "Narrower than the desktop reference" is a viewport narrower than 1600 px (04 "Desktop frame 1600 × 1000 reference"), so the rule applies at the iPad (1180) and Surface (1440) widths and in desktop windows narrower than 1600 px. The tooltip is the button's `title` and the screen-reader name its `aria-label`, both the button's own message, as EZD-015 does for its icon-only close button (vault/plans/EZD-015.md, `aria-label` and `title` both `t('common.close')`, "the title satisfies 11"). A `title` is used rather than a CSS tooltip because the header card clips its content (`overflow: hidden`, S03; EZD-011 Step 2). Stop editing's icon is `edit` (S21 board). Share's and Export's icons are not on any board; Josh chose Share `share` and Export `download` (Q-0098 (a)).
- **What is not here:** the field pack tab bar, back-to-EZ-Binder link, offline icon, revision badge and room chips (EZD-1404 row); phone 3D (EZD-1013); client page (EZD-1202); canvas gestures (EZD-207); a signed-in route that shows the shell (none in Phase 0, Q-0039 (c)); reading the user's language per request (EZD-026b); the Stop editing button itself (EZD-126).
