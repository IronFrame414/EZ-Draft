# EZD-245 · Fix: recurring review findings become lessons and plan checks

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-245-1, AC-EZD-245-2, AC-EZD-245-3, AC-EZD-245-4
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **Areas:** tooling, process
- **User-visible result:** every review finding is logged with a category; when the same kind of finding shows up on two tickets, the orchestrator turns it into a planner lesson with a plan check, and the plan-checker gets those lessons for the ticket's areas, so the same mistake is caught before the build instead of in another review round.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: planned
