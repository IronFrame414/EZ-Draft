# EZD-288 · Fix: a failed customer.created, customer.updated or user.joined event is reported

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-288-1
- **Complexity:** low
- **Areas:** auth, testing, errors
- **User-visible result:** when adding a customer, changing a customer or joining a company by sign-in cannot record its event, the action still succeeds and the error reaches Sentry and the owner alert instead of vanishing.
- Phase: 2 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-02 · Status: planned
