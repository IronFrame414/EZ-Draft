# EZD-375 · Fix: an angled wall shows its length 1/2 inch short after rounding

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-375-1, AC-EZD-375-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **Areas:** geometry, testing
- **User-visible result:** a wall typed 12' 6 1/4" at 45° now shows 12' 6 1/2" in the typed dimension bar, and an angled 11' 11 3/4" wall shows 12' 0", instead of half an inch short.
- Phase: 4 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: planned

## 1. What to build
One changed line plus one private constant in `packages/geometry/src/length.ts` (`formatLength`), and new tests in three existing test files. No data, no migration, no schema change, no new export.

Cause (checked by the planner on develop 5a679b59): the bar shows `formatLength(lastLength, PLAN_DISPLAY_DENOMINATOR)` (`TypedEntryLayer.tsx` `TypedBar`, line 51), where `lastLength` is `wallLength` (a `Math.hypot`) of the drawn end points (`drawing-controller.ts` `finishStep`, line 443; ends from `wall-chain.ts` `typedAt` via `resolveTurnDirection`). From (150, 0) at 45°, a typed 150.25 gives 150.24999999999997, and `Math.floor(inches * d + 0.5)` (`formatLength`, line 73) drops the exact half. The same loss hits 09j row 11 (143.75 → 143.74999999999997 shows `11' 11 1/2"`).
