# EZD-362 · Fix: the ledger-copy fixture gives every ticket it blocks an architecture list

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-362-1 (T1)
- **Complexity:** low
- **Areas:** tooling, testing
- **User-visible result:** the gate passes again on every ticket now that phases 5 and 56 are signed off.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: orchestrator (session 12) · Date: 2026-11-08 · Status: ready

## 1. What to build
`scripts/launcher/test-utils/ledger-copy.mjs` (`makeLedgerCopy`) sets every not-done ticket of phase 1 and of each later signed-off phase to `blocked` in its copy of vault/. A `blocked` row is an open status and needs an `architecture` list in tickets.json (`vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs:403,437`, EZD-244); a `todo` row does not. The sign-off of phases 5 and 56 (3cc2fa32) turned their unplanned `todo` tickets (EZD-204, 208, 211, 212, 213, 217, 5601 to 5605) into blocked rows with no list, so the copy's ledger check prints `LEDGER INVALID (11)` and every suite that uses the fixture fails on develop (reproduced 2026-11-08 with `node --test scripts/ledger/set-aside.test.mjs`; EZD-299a's gate). `scripts/ledger/priority.test.mjs` builds its own copy the same way, not through the fixture, and fails the same. The two copies are what change; no code under test changes.
