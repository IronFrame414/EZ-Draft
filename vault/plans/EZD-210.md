# EZD-210 · Wall and Room rectangle tools (split proposal)

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-210-1 … AC-EZD-210-12 (`vault/spec/acceptance-criteria.md:729-740`, stage-0), shared out to the parts below
- **Complexity:** high (each part; reasons per part)
- **Areas:** ui, testing, geometry, canvas
- **User-visible result:** in a project Josh presses W and draws walls by clicking and typing lengths and turns, or presses R and types a width and depth; the walls save, a closed chain becomes a room labelled `ROOM 1 · 120 SF`, a snap marker shows what the cursor caught, and a chain that misses its start by up to 12" shows a gap box with Pick the wall that absorbs it and Keep the room out of square.
- Phase: 4 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-05 · Status: split proposal

## Split proposal

**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket touches about 27 files and needs about 35 tests, over the size rule (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files, about 25 tests; Josh 2026-10-22). Sources of the count:
- the ticket row (`vault/tickets/phases/phase-02.md:16`) and AC-EZD-210-1 to -12 (12 ACs, each with a logic side and most with a screen side);
- two tool state machines (Wall chain: click, type, turn, close, double-click or Enter twice, Esc per segment; Room rectangle: corner, drag or type, grow direction) (S03 Rail tools Wall and Room rectangle; 04 Tool rail; Q-0560; Q-0568; Q-0570);
- the walls and rooms they write go through EZD-120b's model store and outbox, with EZD-205a's room reconcile step ("every wall-changing tool calls it", P205 part a);
- the gap box: derived from the walls on every load (Q-0570), three or more walls, size to 1/16", absorb only for a parallel wall, Keep out of square, pick mode with Esc back to the box, buttons hidden without Edit model (Q-0562; Q-0568; Q-0569; Q-0570; S02 Resolve the gap; 09j Closure, Absorbing a gap);
- the snap marker per snap kind (S03 Snap; Q-0563; P206 `SnapResult.kind`);
- the screen: rail entries and two icons through the icon pipeline (EZD-010b; `HeaderButton.icon.test.tsx` pins the icon list, lesson L-021), W, R and Esc keys, the mounted `TypedEntryLayer` (P209 section 9 note), pointer wiring and an overlay that redraws per pointer move with no React render (11 rule 4; ARCH Editor shell), permission and loading states (Q-0568; S03 Permissions, States), `en.ts` messages, and browser tests for Josh's Slice 4 test script steps 2, 3, 6 and 7.

**Two more reasons to cut at the logic/screen line.**
- EZD-205 and EZD-207 are split proposals whose parts are not planned yet (`vault/plans/EZD-205.md`, `vault/plans/EZD-207.md`; `vault/tickets/STATUS.md:179,181`, read 2026-11-05). The logic part needs only EZD-205a/205b contracts; the screen part needs EZD-207a/207b contracts. Each part is planned after the parts it reads, so its interface contract matches real contracts (lesson EZD-025: a plan written before its dependencies split cites their real exports).
- Part a is pure TypeScript with unit tests only; part b is React, canvas wiring and browser tests. Same cut as EZD-209 (`typed-entry.ts` pure, `TypedEntryLayer.tsx` screen).

The work splits into two lettered parts, built a → b. Each part is planned as its own unit from its section here.

### Part EZD-210a · Wall and Room rectangle tool logic
- **Scope (place):** pure modules, no React and no DOM, in `app/src/editor/tools/` (`eslint-rules/boundaries.mjs` `EDITOR_CODE`: "app/src/editor/ is reserved for the store, canvas and tools"). Not in `packages/geometry/`, which holds model geometry re-run on the server (ARCH Folder layout); tool state is screen state. Pattern: P209 `typed-entry.ts` (pure reducer, never throws).
- **Scope (tool definitions):** a `TypedTool` per tool (P209 C7):
  - Wall: P209 `WALL_LENGTH_FIELD` then `TURN_ANGLE_FIELD` (P209 D-Fields: "EZD-210 reuses them");
  - Room rectangle: Width then Depth, both `kind: 'length'`, `lengthKind: 'wall_room'`, `optional: false` (Q-0570: Room rectangle is a wall and room field; Width across the screen, Depth up and down; S03 Tab);
  - rail entries as EZD-012a `RailTool` (`app/src/components/shell/rail-types.ts`): ids `wall` and `room-rect`, shortcuts `W` and `R` (04 Tool rail; Q-0568 "W and R keys fixed until EZD-222"), labels from 04 Tool rail ("Wall", "Room rectangle"), Wall icon `view_column_2` (board S03, `S03Plan.dc.html:63`), Room rectangle icon `rectangle` (Q-0588 (a)).
- **Scope (Wall chain reducer):**
  - Click sets the start; each next point comes from the snapped cursor (P206 `snap`, with `anchor` = the chain's last point and `referenceDirection` = the last wall's direction, P206 seam "EZD-210 Wall tool") or from a typed commit (P209 `TypedValues` `length`, `angle`) turned into a direction by P209 `resolveTurnDirection` (Q-0560; Q-0570: first wall from the page's horizontal, "where the cursor points" means after snapping).
  - Each segment is committed as one wall when it is entered (AC-6: committed walls are kept). New walls are the default wall, status Existing, on the open floor (P203 `createWallData`; Q-0558; P120a M4 makes `Floor 1` for every project).
  - Thickness: `right` while the chain is open, outside the room once it closes (P203 `thicknessSideFor`; Q-0569 (b); 09j Thickness side rows 1–3).
  - The chain closes when a click lands on its start point (endpoint snap) or a committed segment ends within 1/64" of the start (P202 `pointsEqual`; 03g; 09j Closure row 1).
  - It finishes on double-click, or on Enter while the inline box is closed (P209 D-Enter: Enter with the box closed is `handled: false`, so the first Enter commits the typed value and the second finishes; ticket row "Enter twice").
  - Esc mid-chain drops only the segment in progress; Esc with no segment in progress leaves the tool (Q-0568; AC-6). Esc while the box is open is the box's (P209 D-Esc).
  - Refusals: lengths and angles through P201 `parseLength` and P209 `parseTurnAngle` inside the typed reducer; a segment that would be 0" or over 200' is refused (P203 `wallLengthProblem`; Q-0570).
- **Scope (Room rectangle reducer):** click a corner; drag to the opposite corner, or type Width Tab Depth Enter. It grows from the clicked corner toward the cursor, right and down if the cursor has not moved; Width is across the screen, Depth up and down (Q-0570; AC-12). Typed sizes are the inside finished face and the thickness goes outside, so 12' × 10' is 120 SF inside (Q-0559; 09j Typed length to outside size row 1). It writes four closed walls; the room comes from the reconcile step. Part a's plan decides from S03 and 04 whether a second click without a drag sets the opposite corner, or asks a QP.
- **Scope (writing the model):** one function turns a tool's committed walls into P120b `Change[]` (`{ id, value: ObjectValue | null }`, `schemaVersion` = P202/P203 `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` at build time) and appends the room creates, updates and deletes of EZD-205a's reconcile step for the floor. New ids are v4 uuids (`crypto.randomUUID()`; P202 `levelSchema` and fixtures use v4). Part b passes the result to P120b `ModelStore.applyEdit` once per committed segment or rectangle, so the outbox saves it (P120b; AC-5).
- **Scope (gap box model):** a function over the floor's objects lists the gaps to show, from EZD-205b's closure report: chains of three or more walls with a straight-line miss over 1/64" and at most 12" (Q-0562; Q-0568; Q-0569 (c); Q-0570; 09j Closure rows 1–9). Each entry carries the gap point and size text at 1/16" (never 0", Q-0570) and the actions offered: Pick the wall that absorbs it only when a wall of the chain is parallel to the gap, else Keep the room out of square only plus the Q-0569 (c) line (09j Closure row 6). Because it is derived from the walls, the box comes back after a reload (Q-0570).
- **Scope (pick and keep):** a pick-mode reducer (00e Pick mode: the next click on a wall picks it, Esc goes back to the box; S02 Esc; Q-0570). A pick calls EZD-205b's absorb: success gives `Change[]` (with reconcile), a refusal gives a message key per EZD-205b reason (wall across the gap, wall of another room, a wall left at 0" or over 200'; 09j Absorbing rows 3–5; Q-0568; Q-0570). Keep out of square gives the room change with `closureOverride` who = `ModelLoad.viewer.appUserId` (P120b), when = now, gap = the reported vector (Q-0568; P205 part b, whose save route refuses another user's "who").
- **Scope (snap marker model):** maps P206 `SnapResult.kind` (`SNAP_KINDS`) and point to the marker part b draws, `null` when no snap applies (S03 Snap "Snap marker shows snap type"; Q-0563). The marker is a small cobalt shape per kind, no text (Q-0589 (a)): square endpoint, triangle midpoint, short bar wall face, arc with 90° or 45° angle, small cross increment.
- **Done when:** AC-EZD-210-4, AC-EZD-210-6 and AC-EZD-210-12, verbatim, as unit tests. AC-EZD-210-1, -2, -3, -8 and -9 for their logic: a scripted Wall chain typed 12' Enter, then 10', 12' and 10' each with Tab 90 Enter, gives four walls and one room of 120 SF; 12', 10', 12', 9' 11 1/2" gives one gap entry of 1/2" with both actions, and picking the 9' 11 1/2" wall gives 120 SF while a 12' wall gives the across-the-gap message key (09j Closure rows 1–2; Absorbing rows 1, 3). AC-EZD-210-10 for its model: every `SNAP_KINDS` value maps to a marker.

  - `app/src/server/messages/catalogs/en.ts` (field labels Width and Depth, the refusal and Q-0569 (c) texts; section 6 of part a's plan).

- **Tests (about 22):** Wall chain: start click, typed length Enter, typed turn 90 / 45 / 0, cursor direction after snap, close by clicking the start, close by typed lengths (09j Closure row 1), double-click finish, Enter-twice finish, Esc drops one segment and keeps committed walls, second Esc leaves the tool, a 0" and a 200' 0 1/16" segment refused (09j Typed lengths rows 13, 16), thickness `right` then outside after closing; Room rectangle: grow right and down with no move, toward the cursor in each quadrant, typed 12' × 10' → four walls and 120 SF, typed 12'6" × 10' → 125 SF (09j Room area row 2; test script step 2), 0" refused; changes: v4 ids, status Existing, current schema version, reconcile appended; gap model: 09j Closure rows 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9; pick: Absorbing rows 1, 3, 5; keep out of square stores who, when, gap; snap marker per kind and `null`.
