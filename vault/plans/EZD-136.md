# EZD-136 · On-screen disclaimer

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-136-1, AC-EZD-136-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** every EZ Draft screen (editor, Settings, sign-in, home, phone screens, client link) shows the grey line "For design and planning only. All calculations and codes must be verified by the user and a licensed professional in the project's jurisdiction." at the bottom, in Spanish on Spanish phone and sign-in screens; sheets and PDFs never carry it.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: questions

## 1. What to build

No data, no migration, no money field. Build only after EZD-103 is merged (step 7 edits its `SettingsShell`; section 9: a dependency missing from the ticket row). Tests render with `MessagesProvider language=<lang> messages={catalogFor(<surface>, <lang>)}`, as `EmptyEditor.test.tsx` `withProvider` does. `Disclaimer` is a client component; server pages and server components render it as a child like `SignOutButton`.
