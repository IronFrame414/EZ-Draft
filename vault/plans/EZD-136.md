# EZD-136 · On-screen disclaimer

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-136-1, AC-EZD-136-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** every EZ Draft screen (editor, Settings, sign-in, home, phone screens, client link) shows the grey line "For design and planning only. All calculations and codes must be verified by the user and a licensed professional in the project's jurisdiction." at the bottom, in Spanish on Spanish phone and sign-in screens; an unknown address shows "Page not found." above the same line, in the signed-in person's own language, else the device's sign-in language; sheets and PDFs never carry it.
