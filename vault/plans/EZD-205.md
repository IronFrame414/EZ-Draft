### Part EZD-205a · Room outlines, area, storage and label
- **Scope (schema):** the room schema gains `status` (Existing while walls are, Q-0558) and `closureOverride` (who, when, gap; `null` when none) (Q-0570, decisions 03; 03b rule 5). `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` 2 → 3 with a v2 → v3 migrate step: rooms get `status: 'existing'` and `closureOverride: null`, other objects unchanged. A stored sample `schema/samples/v3.json` is added (P202 Version rule, `migrationChainProblems`; P203 step 5 pattern). Wall data is unchanged.
- **Scope (outlines):** pure functions in `packages/geometry/src/rooms/`. They find every closed space the walls of one level enclose, crossings and T joins included: "a wall dividing a room makes two rooms; a wall crossing a wall joins at the crossing" (Q-0570; Q-0598: a crossing is a join, which EZD-203 `findWallJoins` already reports as `{ kind: 'cross', otherId }` with no `end` or `otherEnd` (P203 Decided Joins; section 3 `WallJoin`); EZD-205 consumes these joins and creates none). Each outline runs along the inside finished face of every bounding wall, from P203 `wallFaceLines`. Thickness goes outside the room (Q-0559); a partition is centred on its line, so its faces sit 2 1/4" each side (Q-0569 (b); 09j Thickness side row 4). Axes are +x east, +y north (P203 Decided Axes). Points compare with `pointsEqual` / `coordsEqual`, 1/64" inclusive (P202 `tolerance.ts`; 03g).
- **Scope (area):** area comes from the outline (shoelace, square inches ÷ 144), in whole square feet with halves going up (Q-0207; Q-0568; 03g halfway rule). It is never stored (Q-0568 "area derived"; 11 rule 2).

### Part EZD-205b · Closure gap, absorb and keep out of square
- **Scope (closure):** pure functions over a level's walls. They find each open wall chain (walls joined end to end by corners, P203 `findWallJoins`) and report:
  - **closed:** three or more walls whose end misses its start by 1/64" or less (`pointsEqual`; 03g; Q-0568);
  - **gap:** a miss over 1/64" and at most 12" in a straight line, inclusive (Q-0562; Q-0568; Q-0569 (c)). The report gives the gap vector, the straight-line size, and the display size to the nearest 1/16" with halves up, never 0": a gap that rounds to 0" reads 1/16" (Q-0570; 09j Closure rows 2–4, 6, 8);
  - **open:** a miss farther than 12", or fewer than three walls; no room (Q-0570; 09j Closure rows 5, 7, 9).
  The gap is worked out from the walls, so it comes back after reload (Q-0570, decisions 05).
- **Scope (absorb):** only a wall of that chain that runs parallel to the gap can absorb it (Q-0568; S02 Decisions: any wall of the room, adjacent or not). Its length changes by the gap, and the walls after it in the chain move by the gap vector, so corners stay joined (09j Absorbing rows 1, 2). Absorb is refused for:
  - a wall across the gap (row 3);
  - a wall of another room or of no chain (row 4; Q-0570);
  - a change that leaves any wall at 0" or over 200' (row 5; Q-0570; P203 `wallLengthProblem`).
  When no wall of the chain is parallel, only Keep out of square is offered (Q-0569 (c); 09j Closure row 6). Part b's plan states "parallel" with the 1/64" rule across the wall. It also says how "a miss in both directions" (Q-0569 (c)) reads for a room with angled walls, or asks a QP question. Absorb returns the changed walls, and the part-a reconcile step then makes the room.
- **Scope (keep out of square):** the room is stored with its outline closed across the gap (area as part a; 09j Keep out of square: 120 SF). Its `closureOverride` holds who, when and the gap vector (Q-0568; Q-0570). The override clears, and the gap shows again, when a later edit changes the gap: the part-a reconcile step compares the stored gap with the recomputed one by `pointsEqual` (Q-0568; AC-EZD-205-4).
- **Scope (event):** `room.closure_overridden` joins `EVENTS` as `internal` (Q-0570, decisions 07), with payload `{ roomId, gapIn, userId }` (S02 Events: room id, gap size, user) narrowed in `EventPayloads` (ARCH Events). The test-author edits `registry.test.ts`: T1 `INTERNAL` gains the name and its length goes 34 → 35; T3 stays `toEqual` on the new set (lesson L-049).
  The save route emits the event once for each saved room whose override is new or changed against the stored row. The stored row is read under the user's RLS before the save; its `updated_seq` equals the change's `baseSeq`, or the save conflicts (P120a M5 step 8). `userId` is the session's app user.
  A room change whose new override names another user as "who" is refused: 400 `invalid_object` with a new `ObjectIssueCode` (P202 section 3), so the stored "who" cannot drift from the user who saved it (CLAUDE.md rule 18). Emit failure is handled as `model.saved` is (P120a Decided Event: `reportError`, still 200).
