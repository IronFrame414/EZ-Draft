# EZD-274 · Fix: a limit-like error with neither meter full is an ordinary failed unit, not a usage-limit hold

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-274-1, AC-EZD-274-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **Areas:** tooling, scripts, testing

Real five-hour and weekly limits are handled as before, and only they send the limit-file notice.
