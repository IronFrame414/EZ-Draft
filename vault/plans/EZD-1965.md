# EZD-1965 · Check: a stopped or timed-out check leaves no processes behind

Seen 2026-11-23: `supabase db reset --local` processes (PPID a `node supabase.js db reset` under pnpm under local.mjs) outlived their check and their worktree by days. local.mjs uses spawnSync with no timeout; check.mjs spawns steps without a process group, so killing check.mjs leaves pnpm, local.mjs and the CLI running. Do not change scripts/loop.mjs (EZD-1943 covers the loop side).
