The test-author makes the edit, and the code-reviewer confirms that no assertion or expected value changed (PROCESS.md section 8, "Structural test repair"; CLAUDE.md rule 5).

Design rule for the code-bound step: each send to one address waits `max_frequency` + 100 ms (`gap()`, `throttle.db.test.ts:166`), counted from the moment the previous `requestSignIn` call to that address **returned**, not from the moment it started. Auth records its send time while it handles call n (R-062: `time.Now()` is taken after the frequency check and before the SMTP send). That time is therefore no later than the moment call n returns. So call n+1's check at Auth comes at least 1,100 ms after Auth's stamp of call n, more than `max_frequency` = 1 s (`supabase/config.toml:236`), however slow the runner is. Keep unchanged: the expected values, `sleep(3000)` at `throttle.db.test.ts:280`, the loop order and the call count (ticket row; CLAUDE.md rule 16).

- **Cause, decided at design: lead (A).** One of calls 2-5 to the member reached Auth less than 1 s after Auth's stamp of the call before it. Auth refused that send, and the app answered "code sent" by design.
  - **How Auth decides (R-062; GoTrue v2.197.0, the image on the local stacks: `docker ps`, 2026-11-11):**
    - A new or unconfirmed user's email reads `confirmation_sent_at` (`sendConfirmation`). An existing user's magic link reads `recovery_sent_at` (`sendMagicLink`).
    - The check runs before the send. It refuses when the stamp + `max_frequency` is after the current time; this is a strict `After`.
    - The new stamp is `time.Now()`, taken after the check and before the SMTP send, and saved after the send.
    - A refusal is HTTP 429 `over_email_send_rate_limit`.
  - **How the app answers:** `requestSignIn` answers `{ step: 'code' }` and sends nothing for every 429 except `over_request_rate_limit` (`app/src/server/auth/actions.ts:173-185`; Q-0174 (a), `vault/questions/QUESTIONS-answered.md:463`).
  - **Why start-based spacing fails under load:** the gap at Auth between stamp(n) and check(n+1) equals 1,100 ms + (time from start(n+1) to Auth's check) − (time from start(n) to Auth's stamp).
    - It drops below 1,000 ms when call n's part before the stamp runs more than 100 ms longer than call n+1's part before the check.
    - That part is the app's `takeSlot` database round trip and the HTTP hop to Auth. For call 1 it also includes creating the auth user and running the before-user-created hook, because `createTestMember` makes no auth user (`app/test-harness/auth-users.ts:29-51`).
    - A loaded CI runner (four Vitest workers plus the Docker stack; docs/architecture.md Test scope (Q-0006): `test.maxWorkers: 4`) gives that 100 ms.
    - Call 6 never reaches Auth: the app throttle refuses it first (`actions.ts:163-167`). The unknown address is refused by the hook before any send, so Auth stamps nothing for it (Q-0174 question text, `QUESTIONS-answered.md:463`).
  - **Local proof of the mechanism:** `app/src/server/auth/sign-in.test.ts:362-376` (EZD-007d, "(b) real Auth, back to back") passes on the pinned stack. Two calls to one address less than 1 s apart both answer `{ step: 'code' }`, and exactly `1` email arrives.

  - The raw CI log (Q-0742's route) is not needed: the mechanism is proven from the Auth source (R-062) and the passing local test.
