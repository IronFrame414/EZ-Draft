## Split proposal

**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket would touch about 17 files and need about 44 tests (part a about 20, part b about 24, counted below). That is over the size rule's test limit (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files or 25 tests; Josh 2026-10-22), which is also orchestrator decision 1 for this ticket. The work:
- **P1, brief and size** (PLANNING-REDESIGN.md 1.2 row P1; 1.3 "Size"): a brief step per ticket, a size rule that a script enforces, and nextUnit sending a ticket from brief to full plan.
- **P3, code-bound plan** (PLANNING-REDESIGN.md 1.2 row P3): the design step stops before the code-bound blocks. A later step writes them only when every `depends_on` ticket is done. Tickets waiting for it are shown, kept out of the questions unit (Q-0716) and refused at ready.
- **P5, phase dependency check** (PLANNING-REDESIGN.md 1.2 row P5): a script that review-plans runs and that the ready check calls.

**Launcher design (orchestrator decision 4): no `scripts/run.mjs` change in either part.**
- **Each step runs as the existing `plan <T>` unit.** That is the planner agent at high effort with 100 turns (`scripts/run.mjs:100-110`). The plan-ticket skill asks `node vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs --plan-step <T>` which step to do, so P1, P2 and P3 are separate unit runs with separate turn budgets.
- **The plan unit's ledger step is unchanged** (`agentUnitOps`, `scripts/run.mjs:288-312`). It keeps setting `planned` or `questions`, assigning Q-IDs and reading the Architecture line. So a brief must carry section 2 with its `Architecture:` line: `architectureEntry` refuses a plan without one for a ticket that has no list yet (`vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs:493-510`).
- **Progress between two steps of one ticket** (`unitProgress`, `scripts/launcher-idle.mjs:216`; `scripts/run.mjs:782` and `:1203`). A unit counts as progress when the STATUS row changes or its key is no longer offered. The P2 to P3 hand-off offers the same key `plan <T>` again when the dependencies are already done. So that run must change the STATUS row.
  - Rule: a brief carries no `**Complexity:**` line. The brief then records complexity `''`, which the records database accepts (`scripts/records.mjs:245`). P2 sets it (`scripts/run.mjs:295`), so the row changes.
  - plan_lint refuses a Complexity line at step brief (part a), so the rule is checked, not remembered.
  - Every other hand-off already changes the row (todo to planned) or stops offering the key.
- **Restart.**
  - Neither part touches `scripts/run.mjs`, so neither needs Josh's yes for a launcher change.
  - Both parts change `vault/tools/*.mjs`. The launcher's code fingerprint covers those files (`scripts/launcher-idle.mjs:131`), so after the merge it restarts itself in place. It does this at a unit boundary, after the running units finish (`scripts/run.mjs:500-508`, `:819-822`; EZD-250 D9), as it does after every tooling merge. No manual restart is needed.
- **The alternative, recorded with its cost.** Separate `brief` and `code-bound` unit names would need new `UNITS` rows and new `agentUnitOps` branches in `scripts/run.mjs`. That means Josh's yes (CLAUDE.md rule 18) plus edits to the eight launcher copy-list tests (EZD-378 plan, line 12). The only thing it buys is a distinct strike key per step.

### Part EZD-384a · Brief and size (P1)
- **Scope:**
  - **Brief format** (`vault/plans/TEMPLATE.md`, a new "Brief" template block above section 1):
    - the header without a Complexity line;
    - `- Plan step: brief`;
    - a `## Brief` section with a two-column table `| Item | Value |` and the rows `Plain words`, `ACs`, `Screens`, `Tables`, `Routes`, `Files (estimate)`, `Tests (estimate)` and `Split` (PLANNING-REDESIGN.md 1.2 row P1);
    - `## 2. Sources` ending with the `Architecture:` line;
    - `## 10. Open questions`.
    - Both estimates are whole numbers from 1 to 999 (L-061 bound). `Split` is `no`, or `yes`, in which case the plan also carries `## Split proposal`.
  - **`vault/tools/plan_lint.mjs`** gains:
    - `planStep(text)`: the header's step, or `null` when the line is missing;
    - `briefEstimate(text)`: `{ files, tests }`, or `null`;
    - `SIZE_LIMIT = { files: 20, tests: 25 }`.
    - The lint rules:
      - (1) At step brief, only the brief is checked (its rows, the two estimates, section 2's Architecture line, the QP rule). Sections 1 and 3 to 9 are not required. A `**Complexity:**` line fails.
      - (2) At any later step, a missing Brief or estimate fails, and an estimate over the limit fails with the size problem, unless the plan has `## Split proposal`.
      - (3) A plan with no step line lints exactly as today (`scripts/process/plan-lint.test.mjs` v1 baseline stays green).
  - **`vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs`:**
    - `--plan-step <T>` prints one line and exits 0, or exits 2 on an unknown ticket or an unreadable plan:
      - `STEP brief` when the ticket is todo with no plan, or its plan has no step line and status todo;
      - `STEP code-bound` when the plan is at step brief and within the limit (in part a the second step writes the whole plan);
      - `STEP split <f> files, <t> tests` when the brief is over the limit or says `Split: yes`;
      - `STEP redo <step>` when the ticket was sent back to todo with a plan at that step;
      - `STEP none <reason>` otherwise.
    - In both Stage 1 blocks of nextUnit (`:1088`, `:1121`), the todo tickets come first and then the planned or questions tickets at step brief that are within the limit (byPriority), all ahead of review-plans.
    - An over-limit brief is not offered. It is left out of `questionsWork` (`:159`) and does not trigger review-plans (`:1091`, `:1123`). It is listed in PROGRESS.md under "Split proposals waiting to be applied", for the orchestrator's plan-ticket ledger step (`.claude/skills/plan-ticket/SKILL.md`, ledger step).
    - No new import in next_ticket.mjs other than `./plan_lint.mjs`. The fixtures that copy `vault/` whole are unaffected, and the launcher tests use a stub ledger (`scripts/launcher/plan-ledger.test.mjs:52`).
  - **Skills and process text:**
    - plan-ticket SKILL.md: run `--plan-step` first, do only that step, and run the brief's own readiness (plan_lint only) at step brief;
    - planner.md: the readiness line;
    - review-plans SKILL.md step 2: the size finding reads the brief estimate (L-027);
    - PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": the split decision is made at P1, on the brief.
- **Done when:**
  - A brief whose estimate is over 20 files or over 25 tests makes `--plan-step` print the split line, and nextUnit does not offer a full plan for that ticket.
  - plan_lint fails a full plan whose brief estimate is over the limit, and passes the boundary of 20 files and 25 tests.
  - plan_lint fails a brief that carries a Complexity line.
  - A plan without a step line lints and schedules exactly as before.
  - A todo ticket, then a ticket at step brief, is offered `plan <T>` before review-plans.
  - The `--next-unit` fixture runs offer plan, questions, review-plans and build in their cases.
  - `node scripts/run.mjs --dry-run` on the merged checkout prints a `DRY-RUN START` line for its next unit, and the existing gate-phase, wrap and repair tests stay green.
- **ACs:** AC-EZD-384-1 (whole); AC-EZD-384-3 (for part a's change).
- **Depends on:** EZD-383 (`plan_lint.mjs` exports and `V2_BLOCKS`, `vault/plans/EZD-383.md` section 3).
- **Estimate:** about 9 files and about 20 tests.
  - Orchestrator-written files: `vault/tools/plan_lint.mjs`, `vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs`, `vault/plans/TEMPLATE.md`, `.claude/skills/plan-ticket/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/review-plans/SKILL.md`, `.claude/agents/planner.md`, `vault/PROCESS.md`.

### Part EZD-384b · Code-bound step (P3) and the phase dependency check (P5)
- **Scope:**
  - **The design step** (PLANNING-REDESIGN.md 1.3, sections A, B, D and E):
    - P2 writes `- Plan step: design` and these blocks:
      - section 1: "Behaviour per role", "Failure and edge cases" and the five database blocks;
      - sections 2 and 5 to 8;
      - section 9: "Dependencies".
    - Every code-bound block reads exactly `None: written at the code-bound step (P3)`:
      - section 1: "Build steps", "Decided for the builder" and "Do not touch";
      - section 3: the whole section;
      - section 4: the whole section;
      - section 9: "Risks".
    - plan_lint accepts that line only at step design and fails it at step code-bound. At step design the Build steps, Decided and Tests rules are waived.
  - **The code-bound step** (P3):
    - `--plan-step` prints `STEP design` after a brief within the limit, replacing part a's `code-bound`.
    - At step design with every `depends_on` done (`depsDone`, `vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs:106`) it prints `STEP code-bound`. Otherwise it prints `STEP wait <dep> (<status>), …`.
    - nextUnit offers `plan <T>` for a step-design ticket only when every dependency is done, in both Stage 1 blocks, after the brief and design steps.
    - P3 writes the code-bound blocks against develop's code and runs the EZD-383 checks (plan-ticket step 3).
  - **Waiting tickets:**
    - `questionsWork` (`:159`) leaves out a planned or questions ticket at step design whose dependencies are not done. That removes the questions-unit reruns Q-0716 accepted until this ticket.
    - The PROGRESS.md table "Waiting for the code-bound step".
    - `readyProblems` (`vault/tools/plan_ready.mjs`) refuses a plan at step brief or design (`plan step: <step>; the code-bound step has not run`), so `node scripts/ledger.mjs status <T> status=ready` refuses it (EZD-383 D-Gate).
    - The questions skill, step 5, says so.
  - **review-plans trigger** (`:1091`, `:1123`): it does not run while any ticket of the phase is todo or at step brief, so it reads every design and code-bound plan together.
  - **P5:**
    - New `vault/tools/dep_check.mjs` with `dependencyProblems(root, ticket, head)` and the CLI `node vault/tools/dep_check.mjs <phase>`. The CLI prints `DEPENDENCY CHECK OK`, or `FAIL (n)` with one line per problem, or `ERROR`; it exits 0, 1 or 2.
    - It looks only at the phase's v2 plans at step code-bound, or with no step line, whose dependencies are all done.
    - The check, against develop HEAD:
      - (1) `checkContract` (`vault/tools/contract_check.mjs`), for the code;
      - (2) for each `export (exists)` or `signature (exists)` row whose path appears in a done dependency's committed Build steps (read from HEAD, not the checkout, because the main checkout carries uncommitted plan edits), the dependency's committed section 3 must have the same name from the same path, and a signature must take the same parameter count (`paramCount`). A name the dependency's plan does not list is not a plan-side finding: the code check covers it, and EZD-385 keeps committed plans true at merge.
    - Plans with dependencies not done are listed as `SKIP <T>: waits for the code-bound step`.
    - `readyProblems` also calls `dependencyProblems`, so a ticket readied after the phase review gets the same check just in time.
    - review-plans SKILL.md gets a new step: run it, report every line as a finding, and give `VERDICT: FAIL` when it exits 1 or 2.
  - **Skills, template and process text:** plan-ticket SKILL.md (the design and code-bound steps), TEMPLATE.md (which step writes each block), PROCESS.md section 6.
- **Done when:**
  - `--plan-step` prints the code-bound step only when every dependency is done, and the wait line, naming each dependency not done, otherwise.
  - nextUnit offers the code-bound plan unit only when every dependency is done.
  - A step-design ticket with a dependency not done does not make the questions unit due.
  - A step-design ticket with a dependency not done appears in PROGRESS.md's waiting table.
  - `ledger.mjs status <T> status=ready` refuses a plan at step brief or design.
  - `dep_check.mjs` fails a plan whose `(exists)` contract row names an export missing at HEAD, or a signature whose parameter count differs from the code or from the dependency's committed plan. It passes a matching plan and skips a plan whose dependencies are not done.
  - The `--next-unit` fixture runs offer plan (brief, design and code-bound), questions, review-plans and build in their cases.
  - `node scripts/run.mjs --dry-run` on the merged checkout prints a `DRY-RUN START` line for its next unit, and the existing gate-phase, wrap and repair tests stay green.

- **Depends on:** EZD-384a (step marker, `--plan-step`, brief rules); EZD-383 (`checkContract`, `paramCount`, `readHead`, `readyProblems`, `vault/plans/EZD-383.md` section 3).

  - Orchestrator-written files: `vault/tools/plan_lint.mjs`, `vault/tools/next_ticket.mjs`, `vault/tools/plan_ready.mjs`, `vault/tools/dep_check.mjs` (new), `vault/plans/TEMPLATE.md`, `.claude/skills/plan-ticket/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/questions/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/review-plans/SKILL.md`, `vault/PROCESS.md`.
