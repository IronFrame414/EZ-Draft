# EZD-213c · Resolve the gap in the inspector

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-213-11, AC-EZD-213-15 (`vault/spec/acceptance-criteria.md:963-964`, stage-0). They supersede AC-EZD-210-8's "small box near the gap" (05 Q-0630 row 22: "phase 5 plans name the merged tests"; the merged tests that change are listed in section 4, E1-E5).
- **Complexity:** medium
- **Areas:** ui, testing
- **User-visible result:** The red gap marker stays on the plan. The gap's size and its two buttons now sit in a "Resolve the gap" section of the inspector. That section shows when Josh finishes a chain that leaves a gap (its last wall is selected), clicks the marker, or selects any wall of that chain. Esc while picking a wall brings the section back. A view-only user sees the size and no buttons.
- Phase: 5 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-09 · Status: planned
