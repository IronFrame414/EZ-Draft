Probe run while planning (2026-10-24, develop at 43b425b): type-checking `packages/geometry/src/index.test.ts` under the geometry config gives two `TS7017` errors, at lines 9 and 10 (`globalThis.document`, `globalThis.window`; no DOM lib, `strict`).
