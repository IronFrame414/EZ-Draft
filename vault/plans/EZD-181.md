# EZD-181 · Fix: a health check that times out reports the error

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-181-1
- **Complexity:** low (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** nothing on screen; when the database hangs, `/api/health` still answers its failure at the 3-second limit and Josh gets the error in Sentry (and the new-error email once the database answers again), instead of nothing.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: questions

If any is missing, or if EZD-169's health catch or T5 differs from the lines below, they stop and report a `PLAN-GAP`. What they will find:
- EZD-150: `readHealth` rejects after `HEALTH_DB_TIMEOUT_MS` (3,000 ms, Q-0285 (a)) when the `health` RPC does not answer (EZD-150 section 3).
- EZD-169: the health catch is `} catch (e) { await reportRouteFailure(e, ROUTE); return Response.json(…500…); }` with `const ROUTE = '/api/health';` (EZD-169 section 1 step 6, section 3).

The gap this ticket closes (QP-1): a hung database also holds the alert store, because `reportError` writes `record_error_alert` through PostgREST on the same database (`app/src/server/errors/report.ts` `recordAndAlert`, which has no time limit). The awaited report then holds the 500 past `HEALTH_DB_TIMEOUT_MS`, and Q-0285 (a) leaves only about 1 s before the release check aborts at 4,000 ms. The fix: the health route registers the report with `after()`, so the 500 goes out at the limit and the report runs after it. `reportError` captures to Sentry before it touches the store (`report.ts`: `captureToSentry` is its first statement), so the Sentry event goes out even while the store hangs.

## 10. Open questions
