# EZD-103 · Settings shell

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-103-1, AC-EZD-103-2, AC-EZD-103-3, AC-EZD-103-4, AC-EZD-103-5, AC-EZD-103-6
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** Settings opens at `/settings` with its header, left nav (only the pages you may see), a status bar and an unsaved-changes prompt; unsaved edits are kept as a draft on the device and come back with a notice when you return; pages built later already open inside it, and a page you may not see shows the no-access line.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: questions

## 1. What to build

Data: none (no migration). Reads only, through the session client (ARCH Server-only data access): `tenant` (`id, name`), own `app_user` (`name`), `membership` head count. No money fields. Drafts of unsaved edits live on the device only (IndexedDB through `idb`, 02 Named tools Device storage), never on the server (Q-0302).

Sources by short ID: S15 = `vault/spec/sections/screens/S15-settings-users-permissions.md`; P101 / P102 = interface contracts of `vault/plans/EZD-101.md` / `EZD-102.md`; ARCH = `docs/architecture.md`; B15 = board `vault/mockups/S15Settings.png` + `html/project/S15Settings.dc.html`; B15x = the S15a–S15h boards (same nav, status bar); NEXT-AR = `app/node_modules/next/dist/client/components/app-router.js` (16.4.2).
