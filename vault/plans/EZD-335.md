## 1. What to build

Fix: the page, not the harness row (ticket row: no assertion removed or weakened). The line goes in a sticky footer at the bottom of the view (Q-0229 "at the bottom of every page"; AC-EZD-335-1 "in view"). The page keeps document scrolling, so EZD-117a's scroll loading (`MoreSentinel`, viewport root, `window.innerHeight`; `ProjectsView.tsx` lines 77-121) is unchanged.
