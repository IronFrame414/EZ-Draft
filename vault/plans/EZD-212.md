# EZD-212 · Move, handles, distance chips (split proposal)

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-212-1 … AC-EZD-212-21 (`vault/spec/acceptance-criteria.md:899-919`, stage-0), shared out to the parts below (each AC in exactly one part)
- **Complexity:** high (each part; reasons per part)
- **Areas:** ui, testing, geometry, canvas
- **User-visible result:** Josh presses M (or drags with Select) and moves a wall, door or window by dragging, by a base point and a target point, or by a typed distance, with snapping; connected walls stretch, a door left off every wall turns red; the selected object shows end handles to resize it and live distance chips to the nearest walls, and clicking a chip and typing 24" puts the object exactly 24" from that wall; on a tablet the typed bar has Next field and Cancel buttons.
- Phase: 5 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-08 · Status: split proposal

## Split proposal

**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket touches about 36 files and needs about 60 tests, over the size rule (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files, about 25 tests; Josh 2026-10-22). Sources of the count:
- 21 stage-0 ACs (`acceptance-criteria.md:899-919`), 9 of them with several numeric cases from 09k (Chip display, Chip typed, Chip partition, Typed move ×4, Typed length, Door resize) and 09j Moving a wall rows 1-3;
- model geometry for four edits: move a selection (walls stretch their joined walls through `moveWall`, `packages/geometry/src/walls/move.ts`; selected openings move with it, unselected hosted items stay, Q-0630 row 11), slide a hosted opening along its wall and re-host it (Q-0630 row 11; Q-0633 G12), resize a wall end (Q-0630 row 12) and resize an opening (09k Door resize), plus chip distances and the chip-typed solve (Q-0630 row 10; 09k);
- a new tool and pointer behaviour: Move (M) on the rail with drag, base then target point and a typed distance (S03 Move), Select also drags and Move's first click selects (Q-0630 row 35), which changes EZD-208's selection controller (P208 D7: "a press on an object that then drags does nothing until EZD-212"), snapping with the snap marker (S03 Snap; Q-0570; Q-0589), a new rail icon through the icon pipeline (EZD-010b; `HeaderButton.icon.test.tsx` pins the icon list, lesson L-021);
- the screen: handles and chips drawn per pointer move without a React render (11 rule 4; ARCH Drawing tools `overlay.ts` pattern), click-a-chip inline entry, typed size during a handle drag (S03 Drag selection handle; Q-0633 G8), Next field and Cancel on every tool's chip on a tablet (Q-0633 G9; S03d E T2, T3), permission states (S03 Permissions; Q-0214), the `office.tools.gap.fix_later` replacement with three earlier-test edits (Q-0630 row 23), `en.ts` keys, and browser tests for Josh's Slice 5 script steps 3 and 6 (`vault/tickets/PHASES.md` Slice 5).

**Why cut here.**
- Both dependencies are planned but not built (`vault/tickets/STATUS.md`: EZD-204 and EZD-208 `planned`, read 2026-11-08). Part a needs only EZD-204's contract (`openingOffsets`, `placeAtOffset` with `ignoreId`, `openingHostUpdates`, `wallEditChanges` host changes: `vault/plans/EZD-204.md` section 3 "dependents" row); parts b and c need EZD-208's (`select(ids)`, `getUi().ids`, `shareReceiver`, `editorRail`, `edit-bar`: `vault/plans/EZD-208.md` section 3). Each part is planned after the parts it reads (lesson EZD-025: a plan cites its dependencies' real exports).
- Part a is pure TypeScript in `packages/geometry` with unit tests only (ARCH Folder layout: "pure TypeScript, no UI"; calculations re-run on the server); parts b and c are the editor (`app/src/editor/`), the same cut as EZD-209 and EZD-210 (pure module, then screen).
- b before c: the chips show "while dragging" (Q-0630 row 10), so the drag must exist first.

Built a → b → c. Each part is planned as its own unit from its section here.

### Part EZD-212a · Move, resize and chip geometry
- **Scope:** pure functions in `packages/geometry/src/` (no DOM, no React), exported from `@ezdraft/geometry`:
  - move a set of selected ids by a delta: selected walls through `moveWall` (joined walls stretch, corners stay joined: 09e; 12 Wall move; 09j Moving a wall), selected openings by the same delta, unselected hosted openings stay where they are and are flagged or re-hosted by EZD-204's on-a-wall rule (Q-0630 row 5, row 11; 09-calc Q-0630 "On a wall");
  - a typed move: the typed length is already rounded to 1/16" by `parseLength` (03g Typed lengths); it moves along the snapped cursor direction (Q-0630 row 35); 0" and 1/64" change nothing (09k Typed move); a result with a wall at 0" or over 200' is refused (Q-0570 "A move or absorb that would leave a wall at zero or over 200 ft is refused"; `wallLengthProblem`);
  - slide a hosted opening along its host (projection onto the host line, clamped so it never leaves the wall), keep it at its last on-wall spot when the cursor is away from every wall, re-host it on a wall within the 10 px snap capture (Q-0630 row 11; Q-0633 G12); a flagged opening follows the point until it touches a wall (Q-0630 row 11);
  - resize a wall from one end: the dragged end moves along the wall's line, the other end stays, joined walls follow, offsets from the start corner do not shift (Q-0630 row 12; 09k Typed length; 09j Absorbing row 2 pattern in `rooms/absorb.ts`); limits above 0" and at most 200' (Q-0568; Q-0570);
  - resize an opening's width keeping the edge at its offset corner (09k Door resize: 36" → 32" at offset 36" leaves 76"); refused at 0" (Q-0630 row 14) and past the corner (Q-0630 "Door offset"; `placeAtOffset` failures `past_corner`, `overlap`);
  - chips: for one selected wall, the distance to the nearest parallel wall face on each side; for one opening, both sides along its host through `openingOffsets`; none for several (Q-0630 row 10); the chip-typed solve (a wall moves perpendicular until that face distance equals the typed value; an opening through `placeAtOffset` with `ignoreId`, P204 section 3); display through `formatLength(x, PLAN_DISPLAY_DENOMINATOR)` (Q-0630 row 10 "Display 1/2", stored 1/16""; 09k Chip display).
- **Tests (about 24) and files (about 9):** unit tests over every 09k row named in Done when and 09j Moving a wall rows 1-3; 4 or 5 source files, their test files, the geometry index exports. Fixtures built from the geometry package defaults (`DEFAULT_WALL_LAYERS`, `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION`; lesson EZD-120d) and the room helpers `roomWalls(width, depth)`, `partition(x)`, `door()` in `packages/geometry/src/walls/test-utils/fixtures.ts` (`door()` is updated to EZD-204's opening schema by EZD-204).
- **Done when:** unit tests pass for: the east wall of a 12' × 10' room moved 6" east gives north and south walls 12' 6", corners joined, area 125 SF, hosted items unmoved; a typed move of 0" or 1/64" changes nothing and 12' west is refused; 6 1/32" east moves 6.0625" and shows 12' 6" and 125 SF; 11' 11 15/16" west leaves walls 1/16" and 0 SF; 188' east gives walls 200' and 2,000 SF and 188' 0 1/16" is refused; a door chip of 24" leaves 84" and 109" is refused; a partition whose west face is at 57.75" typed 60" has faces at 60" and 64.5" and rooms of 50 SF and 66 SF; a door resized from 36" to 32" at offset 36" leaves 76", and 109" and 0" are refused; a wall end typed from 12' to 12' 6" moves the end, 125 SF, start-corner offsets unchanged; a wall size of 0" and 200' 0 1/16" is refused and 200' accepted; chips display 46.75 as 3' 11", 46.6 as 3' 10 1/2" and 0 as 0"; a move that changes a gap kept out of square clears the override (already the rule of `reconcileRooms`, `rooms/sync.ts`; tested through the move).
- **ACs:** AC-EZD-212-4, -5, -9, -11, -14, -17, -20, -21.
- **Depends on:** EZD-204 (row). Missing from the row (all done): EZD-203 (`moveWall`, `findWallJoins`), EZD-205b (override clear in `reconcileRooms`), EZD-201 (`parseLength`, `formatLength`). EZD-208 (row) is not needed here; it moves to parts b and c.
- **The part plan settles:** whether dragging a wall's start handle moves hosted openings with the start corner (Q-0630 row 12 read literally: "Offsets from the start corner do not shift") or leaves them in place; decide from that row or raise it as a QP.
- **Complexity:** high: calculations with the 1/64" tolerance and 1/16" rounding (03g), and joins (PROCESS.md section 4). Test-author Opus 5.5 (calculation-heavy tests), builder Opus 5.5.
- **security-review: no** (pure geometry; no route, table, policy or permission). **Reviewers:** code-reviewer · calc-auditor yes · ui-verifier no · security-reviewer no.
