# EZD-271 · Fix: a usage-limit error is classified by a fresh meter reading; only a full weekly meter holds the launcher

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-271-1, AC-EZD-271-2, AC-EZD-271-3, AC-EZD-271-4

- **User-visible result:** when a unit stops on a usage-limit error, the launcher reads the real usage meters: a full five-hour window makes it wait for the reset and carry on by itself; only a full weekly meter stops it and tells Josh.
