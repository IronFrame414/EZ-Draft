- **ACs:** AC-EZD-208-1, AC-EZD-208-2, AC-EZD-208-3, AC-EZD-208-5, AC-EZD-208-6, AC-EZD-208-7, AC-EZD-208-8, AC-EZD-208-9, AC-EZD-208-13, AC-EZD-208-14, AC-EZD-208-15, AC-EZD-208-16, AC-EZD-208-17
- **Complexity:** high (section 8)
- **Areas:** ui, testing
- **User-visible result:** Select (V) is the first rail tool and is on when a plan opens, for editors and view-only users. A click picks the door, a second click at the same spot its wall; Shift-click adds or removes; a box selects (left to right: wholly inside, solid cobalt; right to left: touching, dashed green); Ctrl/Cmd+A selects everything; Esc clears. Selected objects turn cobalt and hover shows what a click would pick. On a tablet a bar holds Select more and Select all.
