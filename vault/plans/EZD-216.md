# EZD-216 · Dimensions and text (split proposal)

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-216-1 … AC-EZD-216-8 (`vault/spec/acceptance-criteria.md:1002-1009`, stage-0), shared out to the parts below. AC-1, -2, -4, -5 and -8 are split by clause (see the AC map).
- **Complexity:** high (all three parts); reasons per part
- **Areas:** ui, data-model, testing, security
- **User-visible result:** The rail's Dimension tool (L) measures between two points, along a wall face, or as an angle, and the Text tool (T) places notes with an optional leader that print at a fixed paper size. Each room gets its width and depth dimensions automatically and the floor gets its overall dimensions. These follow a wall move, keep where the user dragged them, and hide with the Dimensions eye. The assistant can add text and dimensions.
- Phase: 6 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-11-09 · Status: split proposal (Q-0674, Q-0683 to Q-0687 answered; the angular dimension waits on Josh for Q-0682)

## Split proposal

**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket touches about 35 files and needs about 60 tests. That is over the size rule (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files or 25 tests; Josh 2026-10-22). The parts:
- **Two new stored object types.** `OBJECT_TYPES` holds `floor`, `wall`, `opening` and `room` only (`packages/geometry/src/schema/index.ts:18`). Q-0667 adds a dimension (kind linear or angular, two points or a wall and face, offset, override text or none, auto overall / room / none, its room) and a text (position, text, leader end or none, paper size 3/32" / 1/8" / 3/16" / 1/4"). Each carries `status` and `level` (03f). Every reader parses through `parseStoredObject`, and the save route refuses an unknown type (ARCH Model schema "Read rule", "Save rule"). So the server validation, the editor's parsers (`app/src/editor/plan/batches.ts:42-55`, `app/src/editor/tools/model-changes.ts:29-45`) and the pick index (`app/src/editor/selection/pick-index.ts:85-99`, walls and openings only) all meet the new types.
- **Measurement and formatting.** Linear lengths show at 1/2" (`formatLength(inches, 2)`, `packages/geometry/src/length.ts:68`; Q-0207; S03 "Data shown"). Two points within 1/64" place nothing (03g; `pointsEqual`, `tolerance.ts:11`). There is no 200 ft limit (`MAX_WALL_LENGTH_IN` is a wall and room field limit, Q-0570). Angles show in whole degrees, half up (09-calc Q-0664). Worked values: 09m Dimensions rows 1 to 8.
- **Auto dimensions.** Each room gets its inside width and depth from its bounding box. Each floor gets its overall extents of all walls, per axis. Both are recomputed through EZD-235's derived cache, and the user's offset is kept after a move (09-calc Q-0664; P235 K1-K3, K8).
- **Drawing.** The drawing list already draws one linear dimension input as a line plus its text on the `dimensions` layer (`packages/geometry/src/drawing/build.ts:253-289`; `types.ts:61-69` `dimensionInputSchema`). It has no witness ticks (board S03Plan draws end ticks, `S03Plan.dc.html:101,103`), no angular arc, no text object, no leader and no override underline (09h line 13). The plan feeds it no dimensions (`batches.ts:74-87`). Dimension text is a fixed `PLAN_DIMENSION_TEXT_PT = 10.5` (`batches.ts:30`). Q-0664 makes it 1/8" on paper and sizes every note by paper size at the plan scale (screen = paper × 96 px × zoom; 09m Text rows 1 to 5; EZD-219 gives the scale).
- **Two rail tools.** Dimension (L) and Text (T) (S03 rail; 04 Tool rail rows "Dimension", "Text"; board `S03Plan.dc.html:71-72`). Each brings an in-place text edit (typed override, double-click; S03 canvas "Double-click") and a note entry. `DrawingToolId` is `'wall' | 'room-rect'` today (`app/src/editor/tools/tool-defs.ts:14`), and `DRAWING_RAIL_TOOLS` is pinned by `tool-defs.test.ts:74`.
- **Overlay, move and the assistant.** The Dimensions overlay eye (S03 left panel; EZD-218 "Membership": "Dimensions is EZD-216"). Moving a dimension or text with the move tool (AC-4). The assistant's `add_text` and `add_dimension` (05 Q-0664 "AI commands"; P5604a D-Schema).

**Why cut here.**
- Part a is pure data and drawing: schema, measurement, auto-dimension values, drawing items, paper-size maths and the assistant format. It needs no canvas or tool. Its tests are unit tests on 09m's tables.
- Part b is the two tools and the in-place text on the plan, with dimensions and text picked for double-click.
- Part c is the auto dimensions living in the model: they are created and kept with each edit, moved by drag, follow wall moves, and hide with the Dimensions eye. That is Josh's Slice 6 step 4.
- Each part is under the size rule: part a about 15 files and 23 tests, part b about 15 files and 22 tests, part c about 11 files and 18 tests.
- Built a → b → c. Each part is planned as its own unit from its section here, after the questions below are answered and after the plans of EZD-214a/b and EZD-218a/b exist. Each part applies the planner lessons for its areas:
  - Part a: L-042 (fixtures for the widened `ModelObject` union); L-043 (`formatLength`, `pointsEqual`, `roomAreaSqFt`, `createDerivedCache` by file path); L-049 and L-021 (tests that pin `OBJECT_TYPES`, `DrawingLayer`, the drawing-view members or the assistant recordings); L-050; L-052 (empty, over-limit and malformed text, NaN points, and every caller of `buildDrawingList` and `ObjectIssueCode`); L-058; L-062 (EZD-120d line: build test objects from the package defaults); L-064.
  - Part b: L-021 (`tool-defs.test.ts:74` rail list; message catalog lists); L-048; L-050; L-052; L-054 and L-060 (touch targets for the two rail buttons and any new control); L-064 (EZD-210b lines: snap-safe browser coordinates; `toBeCloseTo` for angled values).
  - Part c: L-050; L-052 (every caller of `wallEditChanges` and of the room reconcile); L-062 (one rebuild per edit, exact recompute counts through `DerivedStats`); L-064.

### Part EZD-216a · Dimension and text objects, values and drawing
- **Scope:**
  - **Schema.**
    - New `schema/dimension.ts` and `schema/text.ts`, strict like every object (ARCH Model schema; Q-0667 field list).
    - Dimension. Kind `linear` or `angular`. Geometry is two points, or a wall id and face (left or right of the wall line, as `thicknessSide`, `schema/wall.ts:11`). Angular geometry waits on Josh (Q-0682, escalated; the recommended (a) two non-parallel walls with (c) a Linear / Angular switch is pending, not decided). `offset` in inches. `override`: a string or null. `auto`: `overall`, `room` or null. `room`: an id or null. An auto dimension also needs its axis, `x` or `y`. Q-0667 lists no axis, but a room and the overall each have two (09m row 8 shows two values; 09-calc Q-0664 "per axis"). Q-0667 calls the field list a schema choice the code-reviewer reviews.
    - Text. `position`, `text`, `leader`: a point or null, `paperSize`: one of the four sizes. `text`: one or more lines (a line break is stored as `\n`), 1 to 500 characters, from the glyph atlas's characters only; the schema refuses anything else (Q-0687 (b)).
  - **Glyphs.** The Latin-1 letters á, é, ñ, ü, ° and ½ join the glyph atlas (`app/src/editor/plan/glyph-atlas.ts:18-23`) and the font table (ARCH Drawing list "Font table"), so notes print on permit sets and the crew can read Spanish (Q-0687 (b)). A note with several lines is drawn one line under the other.
    - Both join `OBJECT_TYPES`, `DataByType` and `DATA_SCHEMAS` (`schema/index.ts:18-45`).
    - The schema version stays: a new type keeps every stored object valid (ARCH Model schema "Version rule"; `schema/migrate.ts:2-3`). If EZD-218a has raised `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` by then, nothing changes here.
  - **Values** (`packages/geometry/src/dimensions/`):
    - The measured length of a two-point or wall-face dimension. A wall-face dimension is measured from the wall's current face, so it follows the wall. When its wall is deleted, the dimension is deleted in the same edit (Q-0686 case 2 (a)); the pure step below names it.
    - The angle of an angular dimension.
    - The shown text: the override if set, else `formatLength(x, 2)` or whole degrees half up.
    - Nothing is placed when the length is within 1/64".
    - Two `DerivedDef`s on EZD-235's cache (P235 K1, K3):
      - auto room: the inside width and depth of the room boundary's axis-aligned bounding box (09-calc Q-0664; boundary per `schema/room.ts:16`);
      - auto overall: the outer extents of all walls of the floor per axis, wall thickness included ("outer", Q-0664; wall faces from `thicknessSide` and `thickness`).
    - A pure `autoDimensionChanges(objects, level)` that names the auto dimensions to add or remove: exactly one per room and axis, and one per floor and axis when the floor has walls. Its status rule: an auto room dimension takes its room's status, and the auto overall dimension is Existing (Q-0667). The same step also removes every wall-face dimension whose wall is gone (Q-0686 case 2 (a)). Part c wires it into edits.
  - **Paper size.**
    - Notes are 3/32" by default. Labels and dimension text are 1/8" (09-calc Q-0664).
    - A paper size becomes model inches as paper × 12 ÷ scale (09-calc Q-0664 "Plan display scale": 96 px per paper inch, px per model inch = 96 × scale ÷ 12).
    - The screen height is paper × 96 × zoom (09m Text rows 1 to 5).
    - The plan's dimension text moves from `PLAN_DIMENSION_TEXT_PT` to 1/8" at the plan scale. The part plan states how this maps onto `sizePt` and `PT_TO_MODEL_IN` (`app/src/editor/plan/glyph-atlas.ts:14`) and which glyph measure is the "height".
  - **Drawing list** (`drawing/build.ts`, `types.ts`). Stored dimension objects are drawn as:
    - a dimension line with end ticks (board S03Plan `S03Plan.dc.html:101,103`);
    - text centred in a gap;
    - an arc for an angular dimension;
    - an underline under override text (09h line 13: "typed-override dimensions underlined"; the drawing list also feeds sheets and PDF, ARCH Drawing list).
    Text objects are drawn as text plus an optional leader line with an arrowhead. Everything sits on the object's status layer for display filtering (EZD-214a). A dimension is also marked for the Dimensions overlay (part c). The part plan keeps P235 K7's `buildDrawingList(document, view, dimensions, options)` signature or replaces `dimensions` with the stored objects, and updates `batches.ts` to match.
  - **Assistant.** P5604a's `assistantChangeSchema` gains `add_text` and `add_dimension` (05 Q-0664 "AI commands"; AC-7). `applyAssistantReply` lists them under `added`, with the view's status as for any add (EZD-214b). `add_dimension` may name a wall by ref for a wall-face dimension. The `D-Prompt` text gains one sentence each. A recording for each is added (P5604a D-Recordings pattern).
- **Tests (about 23) and files (about 15):**
  - schema accept and refuse, each field's empty, over-limit and malformed case (note text: 500 characters accepted, 501 refused, two lines accepted, a character outside the atlas refused; Q-0687 (b));
  - the atlas and font table hold á, é, ñ, ü, ° and ½ (Q-0687 (b));
  - every existing object still parses at the same version;
  - 09m Dimensions rows 1 to 7 and row 8 values (`11' 9 1/2"` and `14' 0"` for 141.5 × 168), plus an overall case;
  - derived recompute counts after a wall edit (P235 `DerivedStats`);
  - `autoDimensionChanges` cases: a new room, a deleted room, a second floor, a New room's status, a deleted wall that removes its wall-face dimension (Q-0686 case 2 (a));
  - 09m Text rows 1 to 5;
  - drawing items for linear, wall face, angular, override, note and leader;
  - assistant schema accept and refuse, and the apply of each new change.

### Part EZD-216b · Dimension and Text tools
- **Scope:**
  - **Rail.** Dimension (L, icon `straighten`) and Text (T, icon `title`) join `DRAWING_RAIL_TOOLS` and `DrawingToolId` (board `S03Plan.dc.html:71-72`; 04 Tool rail). They are hidden without Edit model, like the other drawing tools (05 Q-0664 "Views and permissions": dimensions and text need Edit model; ARCH Drawing tools "absent without Edit model").
  - **Dimension tool** (pure reducer like `wall-chain.ts`, ARCH Drawing tools). Click two points, with snaps applied (S03 canvas "Snap" row), or click a wall face (hover per P207b `getHoveredWallId`/`wallAt`). Then place the line by its offset (S03 rail "place the dimension line"). The angular mode waits on Josh (Q-0682, escalated; recommended (a) click two non-parallel walls, then where the arc goes, with (c) a Linear / Angular switch in the typed dimension bar (Tab): pending). Two points within 1/64" place nothing (part a). Typing while placing sets the override text (S03 rail "type to override text"): it fills a "Text" field in the typed dimension bar (Q-0683 (a)).
  - **Text tool.** Click to place a note. The leader is optional (S03 rail Text). A click opens a text box in place on the canvas; Enter commits, Esc cancels (Q-0683 (a)), Shift+Enter starts a new line and the box takes at most 500 characters (Q-0687 (b)). A press-drag-release draws a leader from the press point (the arrow end) to the release point (the note) (Q-0683 (a)). Empty text places nothing.
  - **Inspector.** A selected note shows Text and Size (3/32", 1/8", 3/16", 1/4"; Q-0664); a selected dimension shows Measured value (read-only) and Override. Built from the Studio shell components with no new board (05 Q-0664 "No new board" precedent), and listed for Josh to confirm at Stage 2 (Q-0684 (a)).
  - **In-place edit.** Double-clicking a dimension or text opens its text in place (S03 canvas "Double-click"; AC-3). Enter commits as one edit. Esc cancels. Clearing an override returns to the measured value (00g "Dimension override").
  - **Picking.** Dimensions and texts join the pick index, so a click selects one and a double-click finds it (P208a pick rules). Shapes go in `packages/geometry/src/selection/shapes.ts`.
  - **Status.** New dimensions and texts take EZD-214a's `newObjectStatus()`, so one drawn in Proposed is New and hidden in Existing (Q-0667; AC-8).

### Part EZD-216c · Auto dimensions, moving and the Dimensions eye
- **Scope:**
  - **Placed and kept.** Every wall edit appends part a's `autoDimensionChanges` for its floor in the same edit, so it is one undo step. This is the same route the room reconcile and opening host updates take (`wallEditChanges`, `app/src/editor/tools/model-changes.ts`; ARCH Drawing tools "Every wall edit goes through `wallEditChanges`"). A room's two auto dimensions are created with it and removed with it. A room's status change carries to its auto dimensions in the same edit (Q-0667). A room or floor stored before this ticket gets its auto dimensions from the next wall edit on that floor; nothing is written on open or for a view-only user (Q-0686 case 1 (a)). A wall delete removes its wall-face dimensions in the same edit (Q-0686 case 2 (a)). Delete skips auto dimensions: with one selected, Delete removes nothing of it; the user moves them or hides them with the Dimensions eye (Q-0685 (a)).
  - **Follow a wall move.** The values are derived (part a), so a wall move redraws them with the user's offset kept (09-calc Q-0664; 09e "dimensions update live"; Josh's Slice 6 step 4).
  - **Move.** Dragging a dimension changes its offset only. Dragging a text moves its position, and its leader end stays where it is. Both use EZD-212b's move and drag and are one edit each (S03 rail "then adjustable"; AC-4).
  - **Dimensions eye.** EZD-218 builds only the Overlays rows whose kind exists, with the Overlays heading hidden while it has none (Q-0674 (b)), so this part adds the Dimensions row (eye and lock; board `S03Plan.dc.html:90`) to EZD-218b's Layers panel, and with it the Overlays heading shows. Dimension objects join the Dimensions overlay in EZD-218b's layer store. The eye hides and shows every dimension, auto ones included, and its lock keeps them from being picked (S03 left panel "Overlay: Dimensions"; 00g Overlay; P218 "Membership"). Texts are in their Phase layer only (AC-6 names dimensions).
  - **Canvas cost.** The auto values come from the derived cache. A wall move recomputes only the rooms and floor it touches and draws once per edit, with no React render per pointer event (11 rules 2, 4; P235 K10).
- **Tests (about 18) and files (about 11):**
  - edit tests:
    - a room rectangle creates two room and two overall auto dimensions;
    - a second room adds two;
    - deleting a wall that removes a room removes its two;
    - a New room gives New auto dimensions;
    - the overall dimensions are Existing;
  - a wall move with an offset set keeps the offset, and the value changes from 141.5 to the new width;
  - drag tests for offset and text position, one edit each;
  - layer-store membership and eye tests; the Dimensions row and the Overlays heading render (Q-0674 (b));
  - Delete with an auto dimension selected leaves it in place (Q-0685 (a));
  - a room stored without auto dimensions gets them on the next wall edit on its floor, and opening the project writes nothing (Q-0686 case 1 (a));
  - derived recompute counts per edit;
  - browser spec for Josh's Slice 6 step 4: move a wall, and the auto overall and room dimensions update; the Dimensions eye hides them; reload keeps a moved offset.
- **Done when:**
  - Drawing a room places its auto width and depth and the floor's overall dimensions.
  - Moving a wall updates them, keeping a user's offset.
  - A dimension or text can be dragged.
  - The Dimensions eye hides and shows every dimension.
