### Cause (planner, proven on 1cfcd699)
- `outputHolds` (RT lines 1452-1460) scans every journal line plus `JSON.stringify` of the recorded `release.json` (line 1455). In T8 case (c) that record holds `sha` = `R.tip`, the real 40-hex hash of commit C2 (`makeR`, lines 80-89; `commitFiles`, `git-fixture.mjs` lines 74-88), which changes every run because the commit date is the clock time. The three `sha256` values come from fixed dump contents and are the same every run (planner probe of the case (c) lines). Line 1636 calls `outputHolds(h, ['403'])` without `stripHex`, so a C2 hash holding `403` fails it: about 0.92% per run (1 − (4095/4096)^38).
- Reproduction (planner probe, a copy under `/tmp`, not committed): C2 amended until its hash held `403` (found in 13 to 292 amends, about 10 ms each); the old line 1636 failed with `(c) no 403 in the output`; with the fix below the whole RT passed 34 of 34; with `log(e.message)` added after `log('record_failed')` in a copy of `release.mjs` (line 646) the fixed T8 failed with `(c) no 403 in the output`.
- The only all-hex needle in RT is `'403'`; every other caller (lines 1594, 1767, 1916, `SECRET_TEXTS` lines 1461-1470) starts and ends with a non-hex character. No other `scripts/**` test scans output holding hashes with an all-hex needle (planner grep).
