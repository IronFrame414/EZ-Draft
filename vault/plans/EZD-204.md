## Split proposal
**Why.** Planned whole, the ticket touches 27 files: 14 source and doc files, 4 new test files and 9 earlier test files that the v4 schema forces to change. That is over the size rule (PROCESS.md section 6 "Ticket size": about 20 files; Josh 2026-10-22). The cut follows EZD-221's: the stored data first, then the geometry and the flag. Sections 1 to 10 below stay the detailed plan, and each part's plan takes its rows from them unchanged. Build order a → b.

| `placeAtOffset` | Returns `invalid_input` when width, offset or near is not finite, when `width <= 0` or `offset < 0`, or when `hostCorners` is `[]`.
