Terms used below. **Heading**: a line `## <text>` or `### <text>` of `docs/architecture.md` outside a code fence (fence rule of `outline.mjs` `markdown()`); its **text** is the line without the leading `#`s and one space, trailing spaces trimmed. **Own body**: the substring of the file from the first character of the heading line to the first character of the next line matching `^#{1,3}\s` outside a fence, or to the end of the file; a `##` heading's own body never includes its `###` children. **Core**: the list on the `Core sections:` line of `docs/architecture.md`. **Open ticket**: a ticket whose STATUS.md status is neither `todo` nor `done`.
