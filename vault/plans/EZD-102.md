# EZD-102 · Permission enforcement

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-102-1, AC-EZD-102-2, AC-EZD-102-3, AC-EZD-102-4, AC-EZD-102-5, AC-EZD-102-6
- **Complexity:** high (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** a person who lacks a switch no longer sees its button, gets "You don't have access to this. Ask your supervisor." on a page they may not open, and is refused by the server; a switch changed in the database shows up on the open page without a reload.
- Phase: 1 · Planned by: planner · Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: planned
