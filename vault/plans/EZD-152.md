**Step 2, `dropRowLines` rule.** Two regexes, both anchored at the line start, both allowing leading whitespace then PREFIX:
- `ROW_START` = `/^\s*(?:psql:\S*:\d+:\s+)?(?:DETAIL|CONTEXT|LINE \d+):/`: a line that starts a row block.
- `FIELD_START` = `/^\s*(?:psql:\S*:\d+:\s+)?(?:ERROR|WARNING|NOTICE|INFO|LOG|DEBUG|FATAL|PANIC|HINT|QUERY|LOCATION|STATEMENT):/` or a line starting `psql:` : a line that ends a row block.

Walk the lines in order with a flag `dropping` (starts false): a `ROW_START` line is dropped and sets `dropping = true`; else, when `dropping` and the line matches `FIELD_START` (or starts `psql:`), set `dropping = false` and keep it; else, when `dropping`, drop it (a continuation line of a multi-line field, or the `^` caret line under `LINE n:`); else keep it.

**D1** (`### Environments and releases (EZD-023b)`, bullet `**Tenant 1**`): append the sentence `A bootstrap that ends without \`ezd_bootstrap_done\` logs psql's last stderr lines (at most 20) through \`safeLines\` (EZD-152).`

**D2** (`### Off-site backups and restore (EZD-025)`, bullet `**Logs carry counts and codes only:**`): append the sentence `\`safeLines\` first drops psql's \`DETAIL:\`, \`CONTEXT:\` and \`LINE n:\` fields with their continuation lines, which can quote a client row, so no release, backup or restore output prints one (EZD-152).`
