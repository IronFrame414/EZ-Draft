# EZD-103b · Settings shell: unsaved edits and drafts

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-103-5
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **User-visible result:** in Settings, leaving a page with unsaved edits (a nav item, the Projects link or the browser's Back button) asks "Save your changes?" with Save / Discard / Cancel, closing the tab warns, and unsaved edits stay on this device and come back with a notice when you return to that page.
