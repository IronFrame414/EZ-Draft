# EZD-262 · Fix: findings records are written under the ledger lock and committed with their unit

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-262-1, AC-EZD-262-2
- **Complexity:** medium (section 8)
- **Areas:** tooling, scripts, testing
- **User-visible result:** when two units run side by side, every review finding the build units record stays in `vault/lessons/findings.jsonl` and is committed to develop the moment it is written; none is lost or left uncommitted.
