# EZD-5604b · Assistant preview on the canvas and in the panel

- **ACs:** AC-EZD-5604-3, AC-EZD-5604-4, AC-EZD-5604-5, AC-EZD-5604-7; AC-EZD-5604-1 (the plain message), AC-EZD-5604-8 (old shape red dashed, new shape blue), AC-EZD-5604-9 (the chip shown) (`acceptance-criteria.md:1883-1889`; split AC map, `vault/plans/EZD-5604.md`); the Accept, Adjust, Reject clause of AC-EZD-5603-10 (Q-0651 (a))
- **Complexity:** high
- **Areas:** ui, tokens, testing, realtime
- **User-visible result:** After the Assistant answers with a change, the plan shows it as a preview: blue for what is added, red dashed for what is removed, with the legend and the new object's number, and Accept, Adjust and Reject under the reply. Nothing is saved. A reply that doesn't fit the drawing says so plainly. If the drawing changes under the preview, the preview goes stale and gives the prompt back.
