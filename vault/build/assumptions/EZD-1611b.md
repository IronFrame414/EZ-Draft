| EZD-1611b | Wire per circuit | The path runs from the panel through each device in the circuit's stored order. Each leg is the shortest path along wall lines (walls joined at shared ends), with each point taken to its nearest wall line; with no joined path, the straight distance is used. Rise: once from the panel's height to the ceiling, then 2 × (ceiling height − device height) per device, with the ceiling height from the device's room (EZD-501). Multiplied by (1 + slack) and rounded up to whole feet. New Defaults row "Wire slack" (percent, default 15, electrical group) | 09i Wire; assumptions EZD-604 circuits |
| EZD-1611b | Test numbers (AC-EZD-1611b-1) | Worked by hand, as for EZD-1611a. Wire example: panel at 60" in a room with a 96" ceiling, and two receptacles at 16", at 10' and 18' along the same wall. Legs 10' + 8' = 18'. Rise (96 − 60)" + 2 × 80" + 2 × 80" = 356" = 29.67'. (18 + 29.67) × 1.15 = 54.8 → 55 LF | 09i Wire |
