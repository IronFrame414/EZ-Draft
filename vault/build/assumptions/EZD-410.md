| EZD-410 | How "left off a wall after a wall move" is known | Read from the model each time, never stored: the run keeps its host wall id (unlike an opening, whose host is cleared), and the screen flags it when its start is not on the wall's line or either face (1/64"), its axis does not run along the wall, or its rows share no length with the wall; also when its host wall is gone or on another floor.
