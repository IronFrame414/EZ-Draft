| EZD-305 | Which PDF lines are walls (a PDF has no layers) | The straight stroked lines of the page's heaviest line weight: every stroke at least 75% as wide as the widest one (plans draw walls with the heaviest pen). Filled shapes, curves, text and images are not read. The lines then go through EZD-302's wall detector as one wall layer, so face pairing, end joins and confidence are the DXF import's | S01 PDF: detect type; fixture room-12x10-vector.pdf (walls 1.5 pt, other lines 0.5 pt) |
| EZD-305 | What is read from a vector PDF | Walls only. Doors, windows, rooms and supplier cabinet labels are not read, so a wall broken by a door or window gap comes in as separate wall pieces (the fixture's south wall reads as 3 pieces) and is fixed in review. A supplier PDF (kind supplier_pdf) is not read here | phase-03 EZD-305 "Auto-detect wall lines"; S02 States Supplier kitchen PDF (later ticket) |
