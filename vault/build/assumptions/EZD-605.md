| EZD-605 | How a switch leg is stored, and how it stays in sync with switching zones | A switch device keeps the zone it controls (optional device `zone`, no version step). Its leg is the lights and fans inside that zone's outline on its floor, worked out every time, never stored, so there is one copy of which lights a switch controls (the EZD-510 "worked out, not stored" row). Zone to leg: a changed outline changes the leg; the zone's Dimmer edit turns its single switches into dimmers and back in the same undo step (a 3-way stays a 3-way); a deleted zone leaves the switch with no leg. Leg to zone: see the tool row | S06 Switch leg; S05 "Zones sync to switch legs"; AC-EZD-605-1; assumptions EZD-510 "Zone data" |
| EZD-605 | How the Switch leg tool works | On the device toolbar right before Circuit, as on the board (EZD-602's Low voltage, Smoke/CO and Fan items stay between Light and it), same paused and hidden rules. Click a switch: it becomes the leg being drawn and is selected with its lights. Then each click on a light or fan adds it, one undo step each: a light inside a zone puts the switch on that zone and the switch takes the zone's dimmer; a light in no zone widens the switch's zone (same room) to the hull of its outline and a 24" square around the light; otherwise a new zone of that square starts in the light's room, dimmer if the switch is a dimmer, and the switch moves to it. A light clicked first gives a hint; a light in no room is refused | S06 Controls "Device toolbar: Switch leg"; board S06Electrical |
| EZD-605 | What is not built | Taking one light off a leg (a zone outline cannot shrink around one point; the zone is edited or deleted on the RCP instead), one switch on several zones, and a switch's kind edit driving its zone (no kind edit exists for a placed switch). Overlapping zones: a light goes to the smallest zone holding it | AC-EZD-605-1; Q-0500 (controls appear with the ticket that builds them) |
| EZD-605 | How a leg is drawn | Dashed (4" dash, 3" gap, the zone outline's pattern) straight pieces from the switch to the nearest light, then each next nearest, trimmed to the symbols, on the switch's layer, while the Switch legs chip is on (on by default, board). The board's curved arcs are not drawn | board S06Electrical legend "Switch leg"; S06 Show chips |
