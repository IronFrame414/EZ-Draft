| EZD-413 | Where seams and cutouts are stored | On the piece, not as new model types: a seam edge carries a `seamId` shared by the two pieces on either side (kind wall, no overhang, no splash, no profile, as Trace's joints), so a seam counts and draws once; `cutouts` is a list on the piece (id, sink or cooktop, center, width direction, W, D, fixture id). | EZD-413 | How a click places a seam | The seam runs square to the side of the piece nearest the click, through the click (its place along that side rounded to the Defaults countertop increment), across to the first side it meets. A seam that would not cut the piece in two, or that runs through a cutout, is not made | S04b "Click across a piece to split it" |
| EZD-413 | Which part keeps the label and how pieces relabel | The split piece keeps its id as the larger part; the smaller part is a new piece. Both parts keep the name, material and thickness. One undo step | S04b "Pieces relabel (A, B, C)"; board S04bCountertops |

Starting sizes until typed: sink 30" × 16", cooktop 30" × 21". A cutout must lie wholly on its top. Clicking a placed cutout picks it; Enter in W or D sets the picked cutout's size about its center. Cutouts do not change area or exposed edge (board totals 56.1 sq ft count outlines only) | phase-04 EZD-413 row; S04b Cutout; board totals |
