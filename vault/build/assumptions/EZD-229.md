| EZD-229 | What "the room inspector" is for transitions (AC-EZD-229-1) | The Rooms tab fields of the room selected alone, since the inspector for a lone room only shows its name and points to the Rooms tab (Q-0679 (b)). | EZD-229 | Where a transition is drawn and how rooms either side of an opening are found | At an opening: across the opening's width on its wall's line; the rooms are those holding a point 1" past each wall face at the opening's centre. Along a shared edge: the coincident part of two room edges, minus any wall whose footprint lies along it. A flagged opening (on no wall) gives none | Q-0704 "One is drawn at each opening, and along each shared room edge with no wall" |
