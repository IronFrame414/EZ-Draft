| EZD-1973 | What loses the first tap after a touch drag on the rail (the ticket guessed a "just dragged" flag) | Found by a probe in Linux Chrome (Playwright 1.64.1 image; not on a Mac): for a tap that starts within about 100 ms of a touch drag that ended with the finger still moving, Chrome sends pointer down and up but no click (no click at 100 ms, a click at 300 ms and later, and a click when the finger rests 300 ms before lifting). The rail turned a tool on only on the click. Fix: a movable rail tool turns on when a finger or pen is lifted on it without a drag (pointer up); the click the browser may also send for that tap is not counted twice. A mouse press still turns the tool on with its click | AC-EZD-1973-1; CI runs 45375193726, 45463955107; rail-press.ts |
| EZD-1973 | The old "just dragged" flag after a touch drag also swallowed the next Enter or Space on a tool and the next click on Help or Assistant | The guard now holds only for a click on the same tool within 1 s of the drag or tap (by event time), never for a key press, and the next press clears it | AC-EZD-1973-1 ("never left waiting for a later tap") |
