| EZD-607 | Rounding needed to reach 35,868 VA and 149.5 A | Each line is rounded to whole VA, half up, before summing. The demand line is 6,097.5, shown as 6,098. Unrounded, the total is 35,867.5 VA, which gives 149.4 A and fails the AC. Amps use exact half-up on integers: 35,868 × 10 / 240 = 1,494.5, which rounds to 149.5. A floating-point round of 149.45 can land on 149.4. Percent is whole (74.75% shows as 75%). Spare is 50.5 A | 09a table; board S06 |
| EZD-607 | Where the working copy keeps the calc inputs (the electrical_calc row is written only at issue) | On the panel object in model_json: `loadCalc` {method, case, floorAreaSqFt, loads[], overrides, largestMotorId}.
