| EZD-414 | How a new overhang changes the top | The outline is the top as cut (EZD-411), so a new overhang moves the edge out or in by the difference, square to itself; its two corners slide along the neighbouring sides, so a rectangle stays a rectangle (board island 76 1/2 × 27 → 76 1/2 × 37 1/2 with the back at 12"). The change is refused (nothing happens) on a seam edge, when a moved corner ends a seam (as a dragged corner, EZD-413), when a cutout would leave the top, when the outline would cross itself, or when the top would run into another top | S04b Controls; 03c; assumptions EZD-411, EZD-413 |
| EZD-414 | Preset values and kinds | Standard, Bar and Seating read the per-project Defaults (Standard, Seating bar, Seating counter height), Wall + splash the Against wall overhang; the spec's 1 1/2", 10", 12" and 0 while one is not read. The chips show the value in use ("Seating 12"). Flush = flush edge at 0; Standard = exposed; Bar and Seating = seating edges (the board's Default overhangs name both "Seating"); Wall + splash = wall edge at the floor's backsplash height (4" when no top has one), profile cleared. A wall edge left for another kind loses its splash; other edges keep their profile | S04b Preset chips; S15d Countertops; board S04bCountertops |
| EZD-414 | What a typed overhang does to the edge kind | 0 makes a flush edge (a wall edge typed 0 stays a wall edge with its splash); 6" or more makes a seating edge (Round 23 "Seating typical 6"–12""); anything between is exposed. Typed values are kept as typed (to 1/16"), not snapped to the countertop increment | S04b Validation (Round 23) |
| EZD-414 | Which side is Front, Back, Left, Right | A four-sided top uses Trace's side order: side 1 the back, side 3 the front; the diagram stands the front at the top (board) and Left is the side on the diagram's left. A top with another number of sides shows one "Side n" field per side instead of the diagram. The centre box shows the cabinets under the top: the top less its side overhangs, and less its front and back overhangs | board S04bCountertops; assumptions EZD-412 (Trace edge order), EZD-416 ("Side n") |
| EZD-414 | Edge picking and chips on the plan | A click within 10 px of an edge picks that edge and its top (the inspector marks the side); elsewhere on a top it picks the top. Every exposed, flush or seating edge carries its overhang chip ("1 1/2", seating filled "12" seating"); wall edges and seams have none. Clicking a chip picks its edge and, with Edit model and the lock, opens it for typing (Enter sets, Esc or clicking away cancels). While Draw outline, Seam or Cutout is on, clicks go to that tool | S04b Controls "Click edge", "Overhang chip on edge"; board S04bCountertops |
