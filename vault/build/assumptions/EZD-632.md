| EZD-632 | NE2-092 to 105 receptacle placement | Wall line: each room edge at the finished face, broken by door openings (every door is a swing door in the model) and by fixed cabinets with no countertop. A floor receptacle within 18 in. of a wall counts (NE2-095). Receptacles that do not count: over 66 in.; inside a cabinet box's outline at a height within the box; fully switched by the NE2-120 exception; serving a countertop (NE2-096). Countertop top height is the height of the base boxes under it plus its thickness (EZD-411 trace). A receptacle serves a countertop when it lies on the wall line behind it and is no more than 20 in. above the top. Island: a countertop piece with no wall edge. Peninsula: wall edges along one short end only. NE2-101 breaks the run at sink and cooktop cutouts and at range and refrigerator outlines. Fixtures from the check text: 24 in. and 48 in. spacing; 12 in. and 18 in. behind sinks; 20 in. height; 9 and 18 sq ft | catalog NE2-092–105; countertop.ts; 09-calculations-and-rules.md#Q-0559 |
| EZD-632 | NE2-103 and NE2-104 by edition | 8th ed. (2020): one receptacle for the first 9 sq ft or fraction, plus one per further 18 sq ft or fraction. A 9 sq ft island with one passes; a 10 sq ft island with one warns (it needs 2). 9th ed.: an island with no receptacle gives the Checklist item "Provide for a future receptacle at the island", and does not warn. NE2-104: a receptacle 10 in. below an island top with an overhang of 6 in. or less passes at 8th and warns at 9th | catalog NE2-103, NE2-104 edition_difference; 09b E-03 |

NE2-116: a receptacle on the same floor within a straight 300 in. (25 ft) of each New air handler or condenser; 300 in. passes and 300 1/16 in. warns.
