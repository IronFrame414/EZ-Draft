# Phase 13 · Change orders + EZ Binder webhooks and inbound API

Goal: send signed, retried events to EZ Binder, answer its inbound calls, and turn a revision into a change order draft that reaches EZ Binder only on Send.

**Build gate:** against the EZ Binder stub, a revision produces a CO that is sent, and the stub marking it approved locks the matching revision; the Webhooks acceptance test passes (each outbound event is signed, retried on failure, and logged). **Field check (Josh):** a client signs a real CO in EZ Binder.
