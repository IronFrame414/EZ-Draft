- WPB Permits FAQ, "How long does it take to get a permit approved?": "If the submitted documents are code-compliant, a residential remodel permit can be issued in approximately 3 weeks". https://www.wpb.org/Departments/Development-Services/Building-Division/Permits-FAQ
- WPB Permits FAQ lists the Plumbing Chief at 561-805-6692 and the Mechanical Chief at 561-805-6732. https://www.wpb.org/Departments/Development-Services/Building-Division/Permits-FAQ
- WPB Permits FAQ contains no statement on HVAC, load calculations, plan submittal format, PDF or file naming, sheets, or consultant documents. https://www.wpb.org/Departments/Development-Services/Building-Division/Permits-FAQ

## Search-snippet only (not confirmed by reading the page)

- Boca AC Replacement Data Form snippet: asks for sizing support when existing unit data is missing, citing ACCA Manual N, J; asks for AHRI certificate and whether a duct smoke detector is installed or reconnected. https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/391/AC-Replacement-Data-Form-PDF
- Boca Mechanical HVAC snippet: lists contractor registration, required forms and load calcs; subcontractor applications due before issuance; certified NOC for building permits over $5,000 and AC changeouts over $15,000. https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/251/Mechanical-HVAC-PDF
- Boca Electric Water Heater Changeout snippet: lists plumbing application, authorized agent form, water heater changeout form. https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/262/Water-Heater-Changeout-PDF-
- Boca Solar Thermal checklist snippet: asks for a plumbing riser diagram for solar thermal systems only. https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/22473/Solar-Thermal---Photovoltaic-Checklist
- WPB snippet: a separate permit is required for electrical, plumbing, furnaces, boilers, heaters and air conditioners. https://www.wpb.org/files/assets/city/v/1/development-services/documents/building-div/building-division-permit-application.pdf
