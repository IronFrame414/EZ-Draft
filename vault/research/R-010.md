# Findings

Scope: PBC Building Division (in-scope domains only). "Fetched" = text read from the PDF or page in this session. "Snippet" = search-result text only, not opened in full.

## Plumbing

1. Type 2(A) residential checklist (1 and 2 unit), footer dated 10/6/05 (fetched). Item 29 "Riser diagrams." Item 37 "Riser Diagram for Electrical Sub-Permit." https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/Type%202a%20Application%20Sufficiency%20Checklist.pdf
2. Type 3(A) addition/alteration checklist, footer 10/06/05 (fetched only items 1-4). Snippet: item 37 "Plumbing riser, as applies"; item 36 "Gas plans and information per 106.3.5.2, as applies." https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/Type%203a%20Application%20Sufficiency%20Checklist.pdf
3. Water heater like-for-like replacement: self-issued permit on the Water Heater Change-Out form, "for like - for - like water heater replacements only" (fetched). Required fields: fuel type, facility type, pan and drain status, quantity, location; new unit adds gallons, kW, voltage, wire AWG, BTU. Qualifier affirms no electrical upgrade is needed and disconnect is in sight. Cord-connected heater needs an electrical sub-permit under NEC 422. The form has no riser, sizing or venting field. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/PDF/Water%20Heater%20Change-out.pdf
4. Fixture replacement exemption: 1&2 family common fixture replacement on existing supply and sanitary lines, "This does not include water heaters, bathtubs, and showers" (snippet). https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/BuildingCodes/PBC-Amendments-to-the-Florida-Building-Code-8th-Edition-2023.pdf (PDF too large to fetch). Brochure lists sink and water closet replacement in same location as no-permit work (snippet). https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/PDF/AboutUs/PermitReqdBrochure.pdf (not read).
5. Snippet only: 2020 (7th ed) amendments list "Riser diagram/shutoffs" among plumbing submittal items. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/BuildingCodes/PBC_AMENDMENTS_2020_7th_Ed_FBC_FINAL.pdf (not readable in fetch).
6. Not found in scope: DFU/WSFU tables or any DFU calculation requirement for a remodel or fixture swap.

## Mechanical

7. Type 2(A) (fetched, same URL as item 1): item 14 "Equipment schedule with sensible capacity, and plans showing equipment locations and duct system."; item 15 "HVAC sizing calculations if qualifying as renovation."; item 16 "Energy efficiency calculations if the existing structure was permitted after March 15, 1979."; item 43 "Energy efficiency and HVAC sizing calculations, as applies."
8. A/C change-out form (fetched; equipment only): existing and replacement manufacturer, air handler and condenser model numbers, voltage, tons, minimum circuit ampacity, HACR breaker/fuse min and max, wire size AWG, SEER and EER, qualifier signature and certification date. No duct, load-calculation or energy field appears. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/AC%20Change%20Out%20Form.pdf
9. Snippet only: change-out equipment must sit at or above the finish floor elevation of the habitable area. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/Annual%20Program%20Decal%20Online%20%E2%80%93%202024%20AC%20Change%20Out%20Form.pdf
10. Snippet only: recorded Notice of Commencement required if work exceeds $5,000, or is $15,000 or more for A/C change-out; decal program participation $250. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/Annual%20Program%20Decal%20Online%20-%202024%20Registration%20Application.pdf
11. Snippet only: electrical, plumbing and mechanical work each need a separate permit (furnaces, heaters, tanks, A/C listed). https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/Forms/Building%20Division%20Permit%20Application.pdf
12. Not found in scope: a separate rule for duct alteration versus equipment change-out (the only duct item is item 14 above); exhaust fan rules; Manual J named as a method; energy forms for change-out.

## Format and upload

13. Snippet only: Building documents uploaded at ePZB Building > Online Submittal > Supporting Documents, select permit number, Upload Docs; a cover page is required for each submittal; a submittal may be returned at sufficiency review. https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/PDF/PP%20Inspection%20Doc%20Cover%20Page.pdf
14. Plan size 24 x 36 in and architectural elevations in color and black and white (fetched). These are Zoning Division rules (Title 2, 2.B.1.B.1 and 2.B.1.B.2), not Building Division rules. https://pbc.gov/techmanual/Title2.htm
15. NOT FOUND in scope: one file per page, 10 MB limit, 80-character file names, resubmittal name rule. The FAQ the KNOWN line cites (https://pbc.gov/iPZB.Building/Docs/EPZB%20Building%20Portal%20FAQs.pdf) exceeded the 10 MB fetch limit and was not read. The ePZB Portal Basics PDF (https://pbc.gov/iPZB.Building/Docs/EPZB%20Portal%20Basics.pdf) returned binary only.
