# Findings

## North Palm Beach (village-npb.org)
- Checklist index, Single Family Home tab: "Signed & sealed Architectural/MEP plans"; "Energy Calculations"; "Specs for equipment i.e. AC, water heater". Source: https://www.village-npb.org/882/Permit-Submittal-Requirements-Checklists
- AC Change Out tab: "Exact AC Change Out Form", "AHRI Sheet", "Tie down/strapping detail". Source: same URL.
- No standalone plumbing or mechanical checklist on that index; no riser, isometric, fixture layout, sizing, Manual J/S/D text found in the tabs read.
- Plan file rules, https://village-npb.org/951/Permitting-Services-PlanDocument-Submitt: "Only PDF files will be accepted."; multi-page plans "must be submitted as a full set, combined into a single PDF file"; "Architectural and MEP plans, for example, may be submitted as separate sets to retain digital signatures"; files named by house or building number, street, suite, then description, with "[amend]" or "[rev]" suffix; supporting documents "as a separate, combined PDF file"; security settings must match, or plans "WILL be returned". No file size limit, flattening rule or stamp-space size stated.
- Not read (binary PDFs, text not extractable): "PERMIT SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS - SINGLE FAMILY HOME" https://www.village-npb.org/DocumentCenter/View/12134/PERMIT-SUBMITTAL-REQUIREMENTS---SINGLE-FAMILY-HOME ; "PERMIT SUBMITTAL REQUIREMENTS" https://www.village-npb.org/DocumentCenter/View/12304/PERMIT-SUBMITTAL-REQUIREMENTS

## Wellington (wellingtonfl.gov)
- Building Forms & Applications https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/1011/Building-Forms-Applications lists packets: Plumbing https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4943/Plumbing_FINAL-es ; Mechanical https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4934/Mechanical_FINAL-es ; AC Change Out https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4901/Air-Conditioning-Change-out_FINAL-es ; New Residential https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4938/New-Residential_FINAL-es (not fetched).
- The three packets above (Plumbing, Mechanical, AC Change Out) are binary PDFs: not read.
- Electronic plan rules, Guide to Permitting https://www.wellingtonfl.gov/269/Guide-to-Permitting: "Submit files as PDFs"; plan sizes "8.5"x11", 11"x17", or 24"x36""; file names "exactly as shown on the sheet" (examples A-00, E-01, M-01, P-01); "Only one drawing page per file"; the Plans folder accepts "individual plan pages and the pages must be digitally signed"; "All building plans must be submitted through ProjectDox." Not addressed on the page: file size limit, flattening, stamp space.
