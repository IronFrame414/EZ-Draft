# Q-0089 and Q-0237: research for Josh's decisions

All prices were read on 2026-10-28 unless marked. Anything marked "unverified" came from a third-party page or a search snippet, not the vendor's own page. Anything marked "assumption" is an estimate made for this report, not a measured number and not a decision.

## Decisions for Josh

**Q-0089, storage limit per company.** Storage costs very little next to everything else. A typical remodel project is estimated at about 0.5 GB, and a GB costs about 3 cents a month to keep in both places (main copy plus off-site copy). A company that runs 50 projects a year adds about 25 GB a year, which is about 70 cents a month.

1. **Recommended: Option B.** Set a 100 GB limit per company. Warn the company at 80%, 90% and 100%. At 100%, new uploads and new renders stop, but everything already stored can still be opened, exported and sent. Send one cleanup reminder email a month while the company is over 80%, and run a yearly "old projects" review. A typical 50-project company would reach 100 GB in about 4 years unless old projects are cleaned up, which is what the reminders are for. At worst it costs about $2.80 a month per company.
2. Option A: 25 GB, with the same behaviour. This costs at most about $0.70 a month, but a typical company would reach it in about a year.
3. Option C: 250 GB, with the same behaviour and a yearly reminder only. This costs at most about $7 a month per company, and a typical company would take about 10 years to reach it. (A "no limit, fair use" plan, as JobTread has, would reverse your answer to Q-0074 and Q-0075 that every company has a limit, so it is not offered here.)

Two more points:
- Sent permit sets and sent versions are kept permanently (11 Data retention; R24 #4a; architecture.md "Storage buckets and paths"), so a cleanup can never delete them. They count toward the limit for good. You also need to decide whether sent files count toward the limit at all.
- The 100 GB that comes with the Supabase plan is shared by every company, because one database serves all of them. Once all companies together pass 100 GB, the Supabase spend cap (on by default) has to be switched off, or the whole app runs into Supabase's over-quota restrictions. A per-company limit does not prevent that.

**Q-0237, charging other companies.**

1. **Recommended provider: Stripe Billing plus Stripe Tax.** On a $149 monthly charge, fees come to about $6.40 (4.3%). Stripe works out the sales tax, but you (or your accountant) register and file in the states where you owe it. Florida generally does not tax SaaS, so if your first customers are mostly in Florida (an assumption; nothing in the repo says where they will be), there would be little or nothing to file at first. The other choice is **Paddle**: about $7.95 (5.3%) on $149, and Paddle handles all the sales tax itself as the seller. You file nothing, and you pay about $1.50 more per company per month.
2. **Recommended plan: Option P1.** One price per company: $149 a month with 3 office users and unlimited field and crew users, $25 a month per extra office user, and 100 GB of storage. Include a monthly allowance of AI renders, with extra renders charged at cost plus a markup. The pricing options below compare this with per-seat and tiered plans.
3. **Recommended trial: 14 days free, no card needed, with a cap on AI renders during the trial** (for example 20), because every render costs you money. Stripe supports trials with no card.

All prices above are options for you to pick or change. None of them is decided.

### 1.2 Storage per project (estimate)

**What a project stores** (repo sources):
- Renders are stored at full size plus 1600 px and 400 px copies (11 rule 12; 03c `render`: `output_path`, `preview_path` 1600 px, `thumb_path` 400 px).
- Uploaded originals are kept unchanged for the life of the project (11 Data retention). `source_file` kinds are `polycam_dxf`, `polycam_glb`, `pdf`, `supplier_pdf`, `dxf`, `image`, `order_report`, `photo`, `panel_photo` and `field_measure` (03c).
- Exports go to the `exports` bucket: PDF sets, DXF, GLB, USDZ, CSV. Sent-package files can never be deleted (architecture.md "Storage buckets and paths").
- The `media` bucket holds thumbnails, version previews, the logo, catalog images at 256 px, spec sheets, textures and camera-view thumbnails (same section).
- The `jobs` bucket holds scratch input and output files.
- Unsent working versions auto-delete after 30 days. Sent versions and named snapshots are permanent (11 Data retention).
- Off-site copy: every bucket is copied nightly. Replaced or deleted file versions are kept 90 days and weekly database dumps 13 weeks (Q-0075; architecture.md "Off-site backups and restore").

**File sizes used.** No size has been measured in this repo; these are outside figures or assumptions:

**Off-site copy.** B2 holds a current copy of every file, plus replaced or deleted versions for 90 days, plus 13 weekly database dumps. **Assumption:** off-site is about 1.0-1.3x the main copy, and that adds only $0.007 per GB-month. A file a user deletes frees Supabase space at once, but its off-site copy keeps costing for 90 days (Q-0075).

**Per company per year (assumption: 50 projects a year, nothing deleted):** low 5 GB, typical 25 GB, high 105 GB. At $0.028/GB-month that is $0.14, $0.70 and $2.94 a month after one year, growing each year that nothing is deleted.

### 1.4 Options

All three options keep existing files readable and exportable at the limit, block only new storage, and never delete anything automatically (sent files can never be deleted: 11 Data retention; R24 #4a).

| | A: tight | B: generous (recommended) | C: roomy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limit per company | 25 GB | 100 GB | 250 GB |
| Warnings | in-app banner and owner email at 80%, 90%, 100% | same | same |
| At 100% | uploads, renders, photo pins and new imports blocked; open, export, send still work; 14-day grace after first reaching 100% (like Google's) | same | same |
| Cleanup prompt | monthly email while over 80%, plus a yearly list of projects closed over a year with their size | same | yearly list only |
| Extra storage | optional add-on, e.g. per 50 GB | optional add-on | optional add-on |
| Worst-case storage cost per company per month (both copies, $0.028/GB) | about $0.70 | about $2.80 | about $7.00 |

A "no limit, fair use" option (JobTread) is left out because Josh's answers to Q-0074 and Q-0075 say every company has a storage limit.

**Platform limit, separate from the per-company limit.** The 100 GB included with Supabase Pro is shared by all companies on the one project. The spend cap is on by default, and going over quota with it on brings a billing email, a grace period and then restrictions (https://supabase.com/pricing ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/egress). Once all companies together pass 100 GB, the spend cap must be off for the app to keep running, whatever the per-company limits are.

**Egress (downloads).** Not included in the per-company cost above. **Assumption:** at this scale, downloads fit inside the 250 GB per month that Supabase includes, with images served at 400 px and 1600 px (11 rule 12).

Open points for Josh in every option: whether sent files and photos count toward the limit, and what the add-on price is if one exists.
