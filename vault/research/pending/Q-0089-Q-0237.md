## Part 3: what EZ Draft costs to run (added 2026-10-28)

**Josh's answers, as relayed by the orchestrator session and not yet recorded in any questions file:**
- Stripe as the payment provider.
- About $100 a month per company plus $15 a month per extra user. Josh thinks that may still be high: he wants to undercut the big tools by a wide margin and still make a few dollars.
- A 14-day free trial with no card and **no AI renders** during the trial.
- One 200 GB storage cap per company.

This part prices those answers. It decides nothing.

Prices were read on 2026-10-28 unless marked. Image-model prices marked R-014 were read on 2026-10-23 (`vault/research/R-014.md`).

### Decisions for Josh (Part 3)

1. **The fixed bill is about $56-$105 a month whether you have 1 customer or 50.** If Worth Properties' own use counts too (it pays nothing to itself), the bill is about $81-$132 a month.
2. **At $100 a company, 1 or 2 paying companies cover all of it.** With renders not included, each company leaves about $89 a month after Stripe, storage, email and AI text. Even at $49 a company you clear about $40 each, so 3-4 companies cover the fixed bill.
3. **Renders are the one cost that can run away.** A heavy user (Worth Properties' own figure, about 340 renders a month) costs about $15 a month at the spec's prices, and up to about $20 at the dearest models. **Recommended:** keep the S15f allowance the spec already has (Round 24 #18), which refills and tells the owner on each refill. Include a small allowance, about $5 a month, and charge refills at cost plus a markup. "Bring your own API key" is not in the spec and would be a new feature with security work.
4. **Office users in the $100: recommended 3** (for example the owner, a designer and an estimator), with field, crew and client links unlimited. Each user costs you almost nothing (Supabase includes 100,000 monthly users). The options are 1, 2 or 3. JobTread includes 1 user and RoomSketcher Team includes 5.

### 3.5 How to handle renders in the price

| Option | How it works | Effect on the $100 price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1: none included, pay per render | Every render is billed at cost plus a markup (e.g. 2x: about $0.06 fast, $0.30 photoreal at spec prices) | $100 stays all margin (about $89); render charges are extra income | Charging per use through Stripe was not checked in this research (gap). Companies see a second line on the bill |
| R2: small allowance, then refills (recommended) | Include, say, $5 of renders a month (about 100 fast + 13 photoreal at spec prices); after that the S15f allowance refills at cost plus markup and the owner is notified on each refill | Costs Josh up to $5 a company: margin about $84 | Matches the spec's existing allowance and auto-refill (S15f; Round 24 #18). The allowance is set in dollars, as the spec requires (S15f decision 1) |
| R3: bring your own API key | Each company enters its own Gemini, OpenAI or BFL key and pays the provider directly | $0 render cost to Josh; the price could drop | **Not in the spec.** S15f has a model per tier and a company allowance, but no customer key. Today the keys are server-wide settings read only in `app/src/server/ai/config.ts` (architecture.md "AI (EZD-021)"). Per-company keys would mean a new feature, encrypted key storage and a security review |

A local GPU worker (3.3) works with any of the three: renders it runs cost no API fee.
