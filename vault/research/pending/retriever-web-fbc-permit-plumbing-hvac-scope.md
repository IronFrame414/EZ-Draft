## 3. HVAC sizing and change-out
- R403.7.1 (Equipment sizing, Mandatory): heating and cooling equipment shall be sized per ACCA Manual S based on loads calculated per ACCA Manual J; no designer safety factors or future-expansion allowances. Source: search summary of ICC-hosted FBC-EC text (https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/FLEC2023P1) and FSEC comparison https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/commission/FBC_0124/Commission/FSEC_Residential_2023_FBC-EC_vs_2021_IECC_Stringency_Comparison_Final.pdf. Subsection text was not fetched directly.
- R403.7.1.1: cooling-only units limited to 1.15 times the calculated total load, with exceptions (search snippet, same sources).
- Appendix RD forms require an attached sizing calculation (search snippet, https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/FLEC2023P1/appendix-rd-forms).
- Statute 553.9085 is NOT an HVAC sizing rule. It covers the energy performance level display card for residential sales: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/553.9085
- Change-out trigger: NOT CONFIRMED for 2023. An older Florida Chapter 13 document (http://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/publications/Chapter_13_Energy-Efficiency.pdf, earlier edition) says sizing is not required for existing buildings that are not renovations under Section 202. A Commission Q&A says that for total replacement of evaporator and condenser, the contractor must certify minimum efficiency and seal ducts, and that original systems installed under the 1993-or-later code may be assumed sized to code. Both are older text; neither is confirmed for 2023.
- Form R405 is the performance-path compliance report for new construction, submitted before permit issue (https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/commission/FBC_1221/Energy_TAC/Example_computer_run_reports.pdf). No source found that requires R402/R405 forms for a change-out. UNVERIFIED.
- 9th Edition R405 change (e-ratio 0.85, duct testing exception removed): search snippet of https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/thecode/2026_Code_Development/2026_DBPR_Energy_Code_Changes_Overview.pdf. Not read directly.
