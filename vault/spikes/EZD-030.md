# EZD-030 · Spike: wall openings and sloped ceiling

Verdict: go

## Question the spike answered
Can a wall face with a door hole, a window hole and a 4:12 sloped top be extruded to a mesh with no CSG library (11 Performance rule 6; R26 R9)?

## What was tried
`three` 0.186.1 `Shape` / `Path` / `ExtrudeGeometry` (no bevel), with the 2D face built in pure TypeScript (`wall-face.ts`, no imports). Test file `spikes/EZD-030-wall-openings/src/wall-mesh.test.ts` (T1-T13); synthetic 12' wall, 4.5" thick, 96" rising at 4:12, 36x80 door, 36x60 window at 42" sill, plus a falling variant. Details: `spikes/EZD-030-wall-openings/README.md`. The door (sill 0) is cut as a notch in the outline, not a hole, because a hole touching the outer contour triangulates badly (not tried).

## Result
- T1-T13 pass (13 of 13), no CSG library. Holes sit at the opening rectangles within 1/64 in; top edge matches the slope at both ends; mesh is a closed solid, volume 55,080 cu in (calc-auditor re-derived all values).
- W1 mesh: 48 triangles, 144 vertices.
- Bench: mean 0.0061 ms per wall; x 3,000 walls about 18 ms (Node on the build PC, not the iPad), far under the 1,000 ms flag for EZD-1001a.
- Not expressible by straight extrusion: changes through the thickness (splays, niches, different openings per face), slope across the thickness, mitered ends and T-joins, openings reaching the sloped top, overlapping or touching a wall end, corner windows spanning two walls. These are the cases rule 6 sends to three-bvh-csg or a 2D boolean first.

## Decision
Go. `wall-face.ts` moves to `packages/geometry` (EZD-504 wall-top profile); `wall-mesh.ts` goes to the lazy 3D scene chunk `app/src/lazy/scene-3d/` (EZD-1001a). EZD-504 and EZD-1001a planners read this note. Open for them: what happens when an opening reaches the sloped top, nominal vs rough opening size (EZD-204/1001a), and mapping a room ceiling to height-at-start and slope (EZD-504).
